How it works

Just take a screenshot the way you normally would. Readshot detects it and runs OCR automatically, popping up the result. Once the text is recognized correctly, you can copy or share it right away.

Text recognition runs entirely on the device — images and recognized text are never sent to an external server. Readshot is an Android-only app.

FAQ

Q. How do I copy text from a screenshot?

A. The moment you take a screenshot, text recognition (OCR) runs automatically and the result pops up right away. Drag to select just the part you need to copy or share it, or copy the full text if nothing is selected.

Q. What happens if a screenshot has a URL in it?

A. Any URL in the recognized text is automatically turned into a link, and tapping it opens your browser directly.

Q. Can I look back at past screenshots' recognized text?

A. Yes. A history list lets you check the recognition results from past screenshots later.

Q. Is any of the recognized text or images sent off-device?

A. No. Text recognition runs entirely on the device, and images and recognized text are never sent to an external server.
