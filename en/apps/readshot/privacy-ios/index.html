Readshot for iPhone — Privacy Policy

Readshot ("the App") is an iPhone app provided by its developer ("we"). This policy explains how information is handled when you use the App.

1. What the App does

The App reads the text and QR codes in screenshots and photos you choose, or in images shared to it from other apps, on your iPhone using Apple's Vision framework. All text recognition happens on your iPhone. Images and recognized text are never sent to us; we do not run any server.

2. Images

The App does not ask for access to your photo library. It receives only the image you pick in the iOS photo picker or share to it, uses it only to read the text, and does not store it.

3. History

Recognized text and QR code contents are stored as a history only on your iPhone, including text read from the share sheet (kept in a storage area shared by the App and its share extension on the same iPhone). You can delete items one by one or all at once in the App.

4. Memo

What you write in Memo is stored only on your iPhone.

5. Things you open

Only when you press Search does the App open a Google search for the selected text (or all of it) in your browser; Google's Privacy Policy then applies. Tapping a URL, email address, phone number or a QR code's Open button opens the matching app. Share hands the text to the app you choose.

6. Read aloud

Reading aloud uses the voices installed in iOS, on your iPhone.

7. Advertising

The App shows ads through Google AdMob (Google LLC). AdMob may collect approximate location derived from your IP address, records of ads shown and interactions, and device and performance information, for ad delivery, measurement and troubleshooting. The App does not request tracking permission (App Tracking Transparency), does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA), and never passes recognized text or images to AdMob. See https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads. Users in the EEA and the UK see Google's consent form on first launch and can change their choice at any time from "Ad privacy settings" on the Help screen. After you buy "Remove ads", no ads are shown, and from the next launch AdMob is not started at all.

8. In-app purchase

The "Remove ads" purchase is processed by Apple's App Store. We never receive your payment details. The App only checks on your iPhone whether the purchase has been made.

9. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be sent to Google (AdMob) for advertising as described above.

10. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13.

11. Deleting data

There is no account and no data on our servers. You can delete the history and memo in the App, and deleting the App deletes everything stored on your iPhone.

12. Changes

We may update this policy; important changes will be announced on this page.
