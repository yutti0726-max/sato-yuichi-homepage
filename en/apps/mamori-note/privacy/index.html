まもりノート (Mamori Note) — Privacy Policy

まもりノート (Mamori Note) ("the App") is an app for iPhone, iPad, Android and Windows provided by its developer ("we"). This policy explains how information is handled when you use the App. Where something differs by device, the device is named.

1. What the App does

The App lets you create and organize notes, nested folders, photos, handwriting (pen) and checklists, and keep your IDs and passwords in a vault. With Pro, you can sync and back up your data using your own Google Drive or iCloud. You can also choose notes and passwords to hand over to your family in case of emergency, and create a handover sheet or an export file for them.

We do not run any server. Your data is kept only on your devices and in your own Google Drive or iCloud.

2. Information we collect

The App has no way of sending information to us: there is no crash reporting, no usage analytics, and no in-app contact form. The App never sends your notes, photos, passwords or purchase information to us. There is no account to register with us, and the App shows no ads.

Depending on the features you use, however, the App communicates directly with Google, Apple and the app stores (see section 9).

3. Information stored on your device

  • Notes, folders, photos, pen strokes and checklists, together with the text recognized from photos and handwriting for search, are stored in the App's storage on your device.
  • Ordinary notes, folders and photos are not encrypted by the App itself on your device; the App relies on the operating system's file protection.
  • The contents of the vault and of locked folders (text, photos and thumbnails, pen strokes, and recognized text) are encrypted even on your device (see section 5).
  • Notes you delete in the App go to the Trash, where you can restore them for 30 days.
  • If you use a backup of the device itself, such as iCloud Backup on iPhone and iPad or Android's backup, your ordinary notes and other data on the device may be included in that backup. Such backups are handled by the operating system.

4. Information stored in your cloud, and encryption (Pro)

When you use sync and backup (Pro), the App creates a "まもりノート" folder in your own Google Drive or iCloud and stores your data there. Each device uses one of the two, either Google Drive or iCloud.

  • Encryption: Notes, photos and other data placed in the folder are encrypted on your device before they are sent. The App is designed so that neither we, Google nor Apple can read the contents.
  • Google Drive (iPhone, iPad, Android and Windows): Available on all four. The App uses a permission that only covers files the App itself created, so it cannot see any other files in your Drive (see section 6). The folder is visible in your Drive, and contains an explanatory file asking you not to delete the folder.
  • iCloud (iPhone and iPad only): Used for sync and backup between iPhone and iPad. It is not available on Android or Windows. If you use the App across all four kinds of device, please choose Google Drive.
  • Backup generations: Separately from sync, the App stores an encrypted snapshot of your data once a day and keeps up to 17 generations (7 daily, 4 weekly and 6 monthly).
  • When data is not kept: Data in your cloud will no longer be kept if you delete the folder or its files, or if you end your Google Drive or iCloud subscription. Because we hold no copy of your data, we cannot restore it for you. The App lets you know when your cloud storage is running low.

Information handled by Google Drive and iCloud is processed under the terms and privacy policies of Google and Apple respectively.

5. The password vault, locked folders and the recovery key

5.1 The vault and locked folders

  • The vault holds your IDs, passwords and similar items. Locked folders let you lock individual folders. The contents of both are encrypted on your device and stay encrypted when synced.
  • They are opened as follows. On a new device, or in an emergency, you use the recovery key.
    • iPhone, iPad and Android: Face ID or fingerprint, or a PIN
    • Windows: Windows Hello, or a long passphrase
  • Face and fingerprint authentication is handled by the operating system; your face or fingerprint data never reaches the App.
  • Searching inside a locked folder is only possible while the folder is open.
  • On Android and Windows, the App prevents screen capture of the vault and locked folders. On iPhone and iPad, their contents are hidden in the app switcher.

5.2 The recovery key

  • The App creates the recovery key the first time you use the vault or sync. Please print it or save it as a PDF, and keep it somewhere safe.
  • You use the recovery key to start syncing on a second or later device, and in an emergency.
  • We do not hold your recovery key. If you lose both the printed recovery key and all of your devices, no one, including us, will be able to open your data.
  • If someone has seen your printed recovery key, or you have lost a device, you can create a new recovery key. When you do, the key that encrypts your data is also renewed and the contents of your cloud are re-encrypted.

6. Connecting your Google account

  • The App signs in to your Google account only to use Google Drive as the place for sync and backup.
  • For Google Drive, the App uses the drive.file permission, which only covers files the App itself created. It cannot see any other files in your Drive.
  • How you sign in depends on the device:
    • iPhone, iPad and Android: through the Google sign-in and consent screen.
    • Windows: you sign in and give consent to Google in your default browser, and the result is received by a temporary endpoint that runs only on your device (localhost), using the OAuth PKCE method.
  • Sign-in information received from Google is never sent to us. Information involved in signing in with Google is processed by Google under Google's terms and privacy policy.
    • Google Privacy Policy
  • You can revoke the App's access at any time from your Google account settings. Once revoked, the App can no longer sync with Google Drive. Revoking access does not delete your data in Google Drive or on your devices. To delete the data in your Drive as well, see section 14.

7. Searching text in photos and handwriting

The App can recognize text in photos and handwritten notes so that you can search it (a Pro feature). The feature is off at first and is used once you turn it on in Settings. On every device, text recognition is done on the device.

  • iPhone and iPad: Apple's Vision (the text recognition built into the operating system) reads the text on the device. The App does not send usage statistics for text recognition.
  • Windows: The text recognition and ink (handwriting) recognition built into Windows read the text on the device.
  • Android: Google's ML Kit reads the text on the device. ML Kit is used once you turn the feature on and agree on the consent screen.
    • Text in photos: ML Kit's text recognition is used through Google Play services. Google Play services downloads the data used for recognition.
    • Handwritten text: ML Kit's handwriting recognition (Digital Ink) is built into the App. ML Kit downloads the recognition data for each language from Google.
    • Your photos, pen strokes and the recognized text are not sent to Google.
    • ML Kit sends usage statistics to Google. According to Google's disclosure, these include device information (such as manufacturer, model and OS version), app information (package name and version), device or other identifiers, a per-installation identifier, and performance metrics, settings, input and output sizes, feature versions, event types and error codes. For handwriting recognition, the language you set is also included.
    • Google states that it uses this information for diagnostics and usage analytics, that it is encrypted in transit, and that the per-installation identifier is not intended to uniquely identify a user or device. For details, see Google's ML Kit data disclosure.
    • When you turn the feature off, the App stops using ML Kit for recognition. However, statistics collected while it was on may be sent after you turn it off, and downloads of data that were already requested may continue.

Recognized text is stored on your device for search. Text recognized from the vault and locked folders is stored encrypted and is not added to the ordinary search index.

8. Permissions used by the App

8.1 Camera and photos

  • iPhone and iPad: The App asks for camera access to take photos for your notes, and for photo library access to add photos to your notes.
  • Android: The App does not request the camera permission; it asks your device's camera app to take the photo. Photos are chosen in Android's photo picker, so the App does not request permission to read your photos either.

8.2 Others

  • Face and fingerprint authentication (Face ID, fingerprint, Windows Hello): Used only to open the vault and locked folders (see section 5.1).
  • Printing: The operating system's print screen is used. No permission is requested for printing.
  • Other Android permissions:
    • Billing (BILLING): to buy Pro on Google Play.
    • View network connections (ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): to check whether the device can connect and whether it is on Wi-Fi (for example, when downloading data used for recognition).
    • Run at startup (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED), prevent the device from sleeping (WAKE_LOCK) and run foreground service (FOREGROUND_SERVICE): used by the ML Kit components for handwriting recognition in "Search text in photos and handwriting" to download the data used for reading handwriting in the background, and to continue after the device restarts.
  • Internet access: Used for sync and backup (Pro) and the other purposes listed in section 9.

9. Who the App communicates with

The App communicates with the following, depending on the features you use. It does not communicate with any server of ours. The fonts used on screen and in PDFs are included in the App.

  • Google Drive (www.googleapis.com): sync and backup, when you choose Google Drive
  • Google sign-in (accounts.google.com and others): sign-in and consent for using Google Drive
  • iCloud (Apple; iPhone and iPad only): sync and backup, when you choose iCloud
  • App stores (App Store, Google Play, Microsoft Store): buying Pro and restoring purchases
  • Google ML Kit and Google Play services (Android only): sending usage statistics and downloading data used for recognition while "Search text in photos and handwriting" is on (data for photos is downloaded by Google Play services, and data for handwriting by ML Kit from Google). After you turn it off, statistics collected while it was on may still be sent and data downloads may continue (see section 7)

10. In-app purchase and sharing Pro

  • Pro is available in two forms: a one-time purchase and a yearly subscription. You can buy it from the App Store on iPhone and iPad, Google Play on Android, and the Microsoft Store on Windows. Payment is processed by each store, and we never receive your payment details (such as card numbers). Purchase information at each store is handled under that store's privacy policy.
  • Subscriptions are cancelled through each store, following that store's own process.
  • The App handles the Google Play purchase token (information that identifies a purchase) in your device's memory and does not save it on the device or in the cloud.
  • Purchases on the App Store (iPhone and iPad) are verified on your device using Apple's StoreKit. The App does not receive payment details such as card numbers from Apple.
  • Devices linked with the same recovery key share Pro: buy it on any one of them, and you can use Pro on the others too. To make this work, a small record for confirming Pro is placed in the "まもりノート" folder in your own cloud (Google Drive or iCloud). The record is named with a digest made from the purchase's identifier, and its contents (such as the store and the product) are encrypted with the key that encrypts your data. For a yearly subscription, the record has an expiry (14 days). A device that has checked the purchase again replaces the record, and an expired record is not used. The App does not send any of this information to us (we do not run any server).

11. Export, printing and handover

  • PDF export: You can export a single note, or a whole folder, as one PDF.
  • Emergency handover: You choose notes and vault items to hand over to your family, and create a handover sheet (print or PDF) and an export file encrypted with a passphrase.
    • A handover sheet that includes the contents is itself sensitive information. Please store it with great care.
    • You can also choose a version that leaves out the contents and only shows how to open the export file and its passphrase.
  • Manual export: You can export your data as a single file encrypted with a passphrase.
  • PDF export and handover export remove extra information attached to photos, such as their location.
  • You decide where exported files are saved or sent, and you are responsible for looking after them and any printed copies. Files saved outside the App and printed paper are not deleted when you delete the App.

12. Sharing with third parties

We do not sell personal information. As we receive no information through the App, we have none to pass on to any third party. As described in section 9, the App communicates directly with Google, Apple and the app stores depending on the features you use. Data placed in your cloud is encrypted on your device before it is sent. On Android, if you turn on "Search text in photos and handwriting", ML Kit sends usage statistics to Google as described in section 7.

13. Children

The App is not directed at children. No personal information about any user, including children, is collected through the App.

14. Deleting data

We do not hold your data, so you delete it yourself in the following ways.

  • Delete in the App: Deleted notes go to the Trash, where you can restore them for 30 days.
  • Delete the App: Data stored on the device is deleted. If you use a backup of the device itself, your data may be included in that backup (see section 3). On iPhone, iPad and Windows, keys and sign-in information may remain in the operating system's key storage (such as the keychain) even after you delete the App. The contents of your notes and other data do not remain there.
  • Delete the cloud folder: Delete the "まもりノート" folder in your Google Drive or iCloud. If a device that uses sync still has the App, the folder may be recreated from the data remaining on that device. To delete your cloud data as well, first do the following for your storage, and then delete the folder.
    • Google Drive: delete the App on all of your devices, or revoke the App's access in your Google account. A folder deleted in Google Drive is moved to the Drive trash. To delete it completely, delete it from the Drive trash as well.
    • iCloud: delete the App on all of your iPhones and iPads.
  • Revoke access in your Google account: The App will no longer be able to use Google Drive (see section 6).

Please note:

  • Even after you delete a note in the App, backup generations created earlier in your cloud may still contain it. Backup generations are kept inside the "まもりノート" folder and are deleted along with the folder.
  • We cannot restore data that you have deleted from your cloud, or data lost because you ended your cloud subscription.
  • If you lose both the printed recovery key and all of your devices, no one can open your data, even if it still exists in your cloud (see section 5.2).
