Mamori Note | Notes, Handwriting & Password Vault App

Updated: November 16, 2026

Mamori Note (まもりノート in Japanese) is a note app for organizing notes, checklists, photos and handwriting in nested folders. With Pro, you can also use a vault for IDs and passwords, locked folders that you can lock one by one, sync and backup using your own Google Drive or iCloud, and a just-in-case handover that lets you choose notes to pass on to your family. There are no ads.

In short, the developer runs no servers: your data is kept only on your devices and in your own Google Drive or iCloud.

Who is this for?

This app may suit you if you:

  • want to organize notes, photos and handwriting in folders inside folders
  • like to write notes by hand with a pen (with a pressure-sensitive pen, the line width changes with pressure)
  • want to keep your IDs and passwords in the same app as your notes (the vault is Pro)
  • want your data on your own devices and in your own Google Drive or iCloud, not on the developer's servers
  • want to choose and gather notes and passwords to pass on to your family in an emergency (creating a handover is Pro)
  • prefer larger text and buttons (Easy view)

Our approach to privacy

  • The developer runs no servers. Your data is kept only on your devices and in your own Google Drive or iCloud.
  • The app does not send your notes, photos, passwords or purchase information to the developer. There is no account to register with the developer, and there are no ads.
  • Data placed in the cloud is encrypted on your device before it is sent, designed so that the developer, Google and Apple cannot read it.
  • The contents of the vault and locked folders are encrypted on your device as well.
  • Google Drive is used with a permission that only covers files the app created, so the app cannot see your other Drive files.
  • Text in photos and handwriting is recognized on your device on every platform.
  • On Android, Google components send information to Google: the Google Play purchase component sends diagnostic logs each time the app starts, and if you turn on text search in photos and handwriting, ML Kit sends usage statistics (your photos, handwriting and recognized text are not sent).
  • Cloud data will no longer be kept if you delete the folder or its files, or end your Google Drive or iCloud plan. The developer does not hold copies of your data, so it cannot restore it for you.

For details, see the Mamori Note privacy policy.

About the recovery key

The app creates a recovery key the first time you use the vault or sync. You use it to start syncing on a second or later device, and in an emergency. Print it or save it as a PDF, and keep it safe. The developer does not have your recovery key. If you lose both the printed key and all your devices, no one, including the developer, can open your data.

How it works

Once you install the app, you can start making notes, checklists, photos and handwriting right away and organize them in folders. To use sync with Pro, choose Google Drive or iCloud as the destination (one per device). A recovery key is created the first time you use the vault or sync, so print it and keep it safe. On a second device, use the recovery key to start syncing.

Mamori Note is an app for iPhone, iPad, Android and Windows. The Windows version is now available on the Microsoft Store. The Android and iPhone/iPad versions are in preparation. iCloud sync is available only on iPhone and iPad; to sync across all four device types, use Google Drive. The app is available in Japanese, English, Simplified Chinese and Traditional Chinese. It follows your device language and uses English for other languages. The app is not intended for children.

FAQ

Q. Is it free?

A. It is free to download. Notes, folders, checklists, photos, handwriting, search, Easy view and export are free. The password vault, sync and backup, PDF export, creating a handover and some other features are Pro (an in-app purchase: one-time or yearly subscription). There are no ads.

Q. What happens to my data if Pro ends?

A. Your notes, photos and passwords stay as they are. You can still view, copy and unlock the vault and locked folders, and restore from backup versions. Sync and automatic backups stop.

Q. Can the developer see my notes or passwords?

A. The developer runs no servers, and the app does not send your notes, photos or passwords to the developer. Data placed in the cloud is encrypted on your device before it is sent, designed so that the developer, Google and Apple cannot read it.

Q. What if I lose my recovery key?

A. The developer does not have your recovery key, so if you lose both the printed key and all your devices, no one can open your data. If someone has seen the printed key, or you have lost a device, you can create a new recovery key.

Q. Can I use it on iPhone or Android?

A. Mamori Note is an app for iPhone, iPad, Android and Windows. The Windows version is now available on the Microsoft Store. The Android and iPhone/iPad versions are in preparation.

Q. Can I use Pro bought on one device on my other devices?

A. Yes, on devices connected with the same recovery key and syncing to the same destination (Google Drive or iCloud), Pro bought on any one of them can be used on the others.
