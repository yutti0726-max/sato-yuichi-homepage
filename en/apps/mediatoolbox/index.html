Who is this for?

MediaToolbox is a good fit if you've run into any of these on your phone:

  • A video file too large to send over LINE or email
  • A video format that won't play, and you need it in standard MP4
  • Wanting to resize an image (width/height) right on your phone
  • Needing to convert between image formats (JPEG/PNG/WEBP)
  • Wanting to turn a PDF into images, or images into a PDF
  • Needing to pull just the audio out of a video

How it works

The basic flow is the same across every feature: open the app, pick the file (video, image, or PDF) you want to convert, set the output condition (resolution, size, or format), and tap "Convert & Save." Step-by-step guides for each feature are linked below.

MediaToolbox is an Android-only app. It is not available for iPhone (iOS).

FAQ

Q. Is MediaToolbox free to use?

A. Yes. You can download it for free from Google Play.

Q. Can I convert video without a computer?

A. Yes. Resolution changes, MP4 conversion, and compression can all be done entirely on your Android phone.

Q. Can I resize or convert images too?

A. Yes. You can resize by width and height (with an optional aspect-ratio lock), and convert between JPEG, PNG, and WEBP.

Q. Does it handle PDF conversion?

A. Yes. You can export every page of a PDF as a JPEG or PNG, or build a PDF from images.

Q. Can I extract just the audio from a video?

A. Yes. You can extract just the audio track from a video and save it as an M4A file.
