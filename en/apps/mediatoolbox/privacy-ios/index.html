MediaToolbox for iPhone — Privacy Policy

MediaToolbox ("the App") is an iPhone app provided by its developer ("we"). This policy explains how information is handled when you use the App.

1. What the App does

The App converts the videos, images and PDFs you choose, on your iPhone: video resolution, image size and format, audio from video, and PDF to images and back. All conversion happens on your iPhone. We do not run any server, and your files are never sent to us.

2. Files you choose

The App reads only the files you pick in the iOS photo picker, the Files picker or the share sheet, and uses them only for the conversion. These files and intermediate files are kept in the App's temporary storage and deleted the next time the App starts. Files sent to the App from the share sheet wait, until you open the App, in a storage area on your iPhone that only the App and its share extension can use (an App Group); they are moved to the temporary storage when the App opens. The photo picker shows the App only the photos you select.

3. Saving to Photos

To save converted videos and images, the App asks for permission to add to your Photos library. It does not read your library. PDFs and audio files go only where you choose with "Share / Save". You can change this permission at any time in the Settings app.

4. Camera

The camera is used only when you take pictures for "Convert Images to PDF". The pictures are used only to make the PDF and are not saved to Photos.

5. Recent activity

The list of recent jobs on the home screen (type, file name, size; up to 20) is stored on your iPhone and is not sent to us.

6. In-app purchase

The one-time purchase that removes ads is processed by Apple's App Store. We never receive payment details. The App checks on your iPhone whether you have purchased it and stores that result on your iPhone (it is not sent to us).

7. Advertising

Unless you have removed ads by purchase, the App shows ads through Google AdMob (Google LLC). AdMob may collect approximate location derived from your IP address, records of ads shown and interactions, and device and performance information, for ad delivery, measurement and troubleshooting. The App does not request tracking permission (App Tracking Transparency) and does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA). Google's handling of this information is governed by Google's Privacy Policy. See https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads. Users in the EEA and the UK see Google's consent form on first launch and can change their choice at any time from "Ad privacy settings" at the bottom of the home screen.

8. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be sent to Google (AdMob) for advertising as described above. Your files are never sent to anyone unless you choose a destination with "Share / Save".

9. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13.

10. Deleting data

There is no account and no data on our servers. Deleting the App deletes the data it stored on your iPhone; files you saved to Photos or Files stay there. If you use iPhone backups (iCloud Backup or a computer), the App's data, such as the list of recent jobs, may be included in those backups or in a transfer to a new iPhone.

11. Changes

We may update this policy; important changes will be announced on this page.
