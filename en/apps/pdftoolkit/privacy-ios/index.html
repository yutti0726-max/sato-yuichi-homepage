PDF Toolkit for iPhone — Privacy Policy

PDF Toolkit ("the App") is an iPhone app provided by its developer ("we"). This policy explains how information is handled when you use the App.

1. What the App does

The App merges, splits and compresses PDFs, edits pages, scans documents and recognizes text to make PDFs searchable. All PDFs, images and recognized text are processed and stored on your iPhone. We do not run any server, and nothing is sent to us. If you use iPhone backups (iCloud Backup or a computer), PDFs and other data kept in the App may be included in those backups or in a transfer to a new iPhone.

2. PDFs and images

PDFs you create, edit or import in the App, and their names, are stored on your iPhone in the App's own storage and are never sent to us (if you back up your iPhone, for example to iCloud, they may be included in that backup). A PDF chosen from Files is copied temporarily on your iPhone for processing; the original file is never changed.

3. Camera

The App asks for camera access to scan documents. The pictures are used only to make PDFs on your iPhone. You can withdraw this permission at any time in the Settings app.

4. Photos

"Create PDF from images" receives only the photos you pick in the iOS photo picker; the App does not ask for access to your whole photo library. Permission to add to Photos is requested only if you choose "Save Image" in the share sheet to save exported page images.

5. Text recognition

Text recognition for searchable PDFs uses the iOS built-in Vision framework and runs only on your iPhone. The contents of your documents are never sent anywhere for text recognition.

6. Purchases (PRO)

Buying and restoring PRO is handled by Apple's App Store (StoreKit). The App only checks on your iPhone whether PRO has been bought; it never receives or stores payment information.

7. Advertising

The App shows ads through Google AdMob (Google LLC): a banner at the bottom of the screen, and a video ad when you choose "Export without watermark". AdMob may collect approximate location derived from your IP address, records of ads shown and interactions, and device and performance information, for ad delivery, measurement and troubleshooting. The App does not request tracking permission (App Tracking Transparency) and does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA). Google's handling of this information is governed by Google's Privacy Policy. See https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads. With PRO, no ads are shown. Users in the EEA and the UK see Google's consent form on first launch and can change their choice at any time from "Ad privacy settings" in the App's Settings.

8. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be sent to Google (AdMob) for advertising as described above.

9. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13.

10. Deleting data

There is no account and no data on our servers. "Delete all data" in the App's Settings deletes the PDFs stored on your iPhone, and deleting the App deletes everything.

11. Changes

We may update this policy; important changes will be announced on this page.
