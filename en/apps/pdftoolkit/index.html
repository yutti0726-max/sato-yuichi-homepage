PDF Toolkit | Scan, Merge, Split & OCR — Free PDF App

Updated: September 14, 2026

Scanning a paper document, merging several PDFs into one, pulling out just the pages you need — PDF tasks come in many shapes, and hunting for a different app for each one gets old fast. PDF Toolkit brings the everyday PDF tasks together into one free app.

In short: with PDF Toolkit, scanning, OCR, merging, splitting, and signing can all be done entirely on your phone.

PDF Toolkit

Scan, merge, split, compress, and recognize text with OCR — the everyday PDF tasks, all in one app. Great for anyone dealing with contracts, invoices, or lots of paperwork.

Who is this for?

PDF Toolkit is a good fit if you've run into any of these:

  • Wanting to scan a paper document into a PDF, without a scanner or all-in-one printer
  • Needing to merge multiple PDFs into one, or split out just the pages you need
  • A PDF file too large to send
  • Wanting to make a scanned PDF's text searchable and copyable (OCR)
  • Needing to add a handwritten signature or note to a contract or form
  • A PDF with pages facing the wrong way that need straightening out

How it works

Open the app, pick the PDF (or a saved/captured photo), and tap the feature you want (merge, split, OCR, and so on). Scanning is as simple as pointing the camera at a document — cropping and deskewing happen automatically. Step-by-step guides for each feature are linked below.

PDF Toolkit is an Android-only app. It is not available for iPhone (iOS).

FAQ

Q. Is PDF Toolkit free to use?

A. Yes. You can download it for free from Google Play.

Q. Can I scan a paper document into a PDF on my phone?

A. Yes. Photograph the document and it's auto-corrected and saved as a PDF.

Q. Can I merge multiple PDFs into one?

A. Yes, and you can also split a PDF by page range.

Q. Can I make a scanned PDF's text searchable and copyable?

A. Yes, with OCR — it recognizes the text in a scanned PDF so you can search and copy it.

Q. Can I add a handwritten signature to a PDF?

A. Yes. You can add a signature or note drawn with your finger directly onto a PDF, such as a contract.
