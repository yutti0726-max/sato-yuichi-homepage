Rakuraku Step Counter | No-Button Pedometer for Seniors

Updated: September 14, 2026

Tried a step-counting app before and gave up because it was too fiddly? Rakuraku Step Counter ("easy step counter") tracks your steps automatically with no buttons to press — built to be easy for seniors to use.

In short: install it, grant one permission, and from then on your steps are tracked automatically just by carrying your phone.

Rakuraku Step Counter

No buttons to press — automatic step tracking designed to be easy for seniors. Works well for the user themselves, and just as well for a family member setting it up on their behalf.

Who is this for?

This app is a good fit if:

  • You've tried a step-counter app before, but the controls felt too fiddly to stick with
  • You want steps tracked automatically, without opening the app
  • You want to check today's steps at a glance from the home screen
  • You want a little extra motivation to keep walking
  • You're a family member who wants to set up step tracking for a parent or grandparent who lives elsewhere

How it works

After installing, just grant the one permission needed for step tracking (physical activity) and you're set up. From there, steps are counted automatically as you carry your phone around. Add the widget to your home screen and today's step count is visible at a glance, no app-opening required.

Rakuraku Step Counter is an Android-only app. It is not available for iPhone (iOS).

FAQ

Q. Is Rakuraku Step Counter free to use?

A. Yes. You can download it for free from Google Play.

Q. Do I need to open the app every day for it to track steps?

A. No. It tracks your steps automatically in the background, even without opening the app.

Q. Can I check my steps from the home screen?

A. Yes. A home-screen widget shows today's steps and calories in large text, and the size is adjustable.

Q. Are there features that make it easier to stick with?

A. Yes — a rank system that climbs with the distance you've walked, and notifications when you hit your daily step goal.

Q. Can I set this up on a parent's or grandparent's phone for them?

A. Yes. Tracking starts automatically after granting one permission, so it's easy for family members to set up on someone else's behalf.
