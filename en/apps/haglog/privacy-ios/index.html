HagLog for iPhone — Privacy Policy

HagLog ("the App") is a baby record book app for iPhone provided by its developer ("we"). This policy explains how information is handled when you use the App.

1. What the App does

The App lets you keep your child's profile, a photo diary, growth records, firsts, vaccination and checkup records, and notes, all entered by you. These records and photos are stored and processed only on your iPhone. We do not run any server, nothing is sent to us, and there is no account.

2. Records you enter

Your child's name, nickname, date of birth, sex, blood type, birth weight and length, place of birth and notes about the day they were born; diary text, weather and mood; height, weight and head circumference; firsts; vaccination and checkup records (dates, clinic, lot number, notes); and notes. They are stored only in the App's storage on your iPhone and used only to show your records and to make the PDF you ask for.

3. Photos

Only photos you choose in the iOS photo picker are copied into the App's storage. The App does not ask for access to your whole photo library. Photos are used only in the diary, the profile, firsts and the PDF you make.

4. Face ID (app lock)

If you turn on the app lock, the App uses iOS Face ID (or your iPhone passcode) to unlock. Face data is handled by iOS and never reaches the App.

5. PDF export

The memory-book PDF is made only when you ask, and goes only where you choose in the share sheet (Files, printing, another app and so on).

6. Advertising

The App shows ads through Google AdMob (Google LLC). AdMob may collect approximate location derived from your IP address, records of ads shown and interactions, and device and performance information, for ad delivery, measurement and troubleshooting. The App does not pass the records or photos above to AdMob. The App does not request tracking permission (App Tracking Transparency) and does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA). Google's handling of this information is governed by Google's Privacy Policy. See https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads. Users in the EEA and the UK see Google's consent form on first launch and can change their choice at any time from "Ad privacy settings" in the Settings tab.

7. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be sent to Google (AdMob) for advertising as described above.

8. Children's information

The App is for parents and guardians to keep records of their children; it is not meant to be used by children. Your child's information is entered by you, stays on your iPhone and is never collected by us.

9. Storage and deletion

Records and photos are stored only on your iPhone. If you use full iPhone backups (iCloud Backup or a computer), they may be included there. Deleting the App deletes all records and photos on your iPhone. You can clear or change what you recorded in the App at any time (deleting a child deletes all of that child's records and photos).

10. Changes

We may update this policy; important changes will be announced on this page.
