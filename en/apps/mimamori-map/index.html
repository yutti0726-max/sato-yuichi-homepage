Our approach to privacy

  • Nothing is shared until both of you agree
  • While your location is being collected, a notification is always shown. There is no hidden mode
  • No ads and no usage analytics. The app does not access your contacts
  • You can delete your account and your data from inside the app at any time

How location is used

To share your location while the app is closed or not in use, the app asks for “Allow all the time” location access. It uses your location only to share it with the people you approved, and only while sharing is on. If you do not allow it, sharing works only while the app is open.

How it works

Open the app, create an account, and give the person you want to look out for an invite code or QR code. When they approve, you are connected. Your location and battery level are shared with each person only while you have sharing turned on for that person. A notification is always shown while your location is being collected.

Mimamori Map is not an emergency service and cannot guarantee anyone's safety. Updates can be delayed or missing, for example because of no signal, an empty battery, power-saving settings or permissions. It is intended for people aged 18 and over. It is an Android-only app, available in English, Japanese, Simplified Chinese and Traditional Chinese.

FAQ

Q. Can someone see my location without my knowing?

A. No. You connect with an invite code or QR code, and nothing is visible until the other person approves. A notification is always shown while your location is collected, and there is no hidden mode.

Q. How do I stop sharing?

A. You can stop sharing with each person at any time, with one switch or from the notification.

Q. Is my location shared while the app is closed?

A. If you allow “Allow all the time” location access, it is shared while sharing is on, even when the app is closed. If you do not allow it, sharing works only while the app is open.

Q. Are there ads or usage analytics?

A. No. The app does not access your contacts either.

Q. Can I delete my account and data?

A. Yes, from inside the app at any time.

Q. What age is it for?

A. It is intended for people aged 18 and over.

Q. If updates stop, does that mean something has happened to the other person?

A. Not necessarily. Updates also stop with no signal, an empty battery or power-saving settings. The app separates what is confirmed (such as them turning sharing off) from what is only a guess. In an emergency, contact them directly or call emergency services.
