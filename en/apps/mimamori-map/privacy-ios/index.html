Imadoko Share for iPhone — Privacy Policy

The iPhone version of "Imadoko Share" (the "App") lets you share your location, battery level and messages only with people you have mutually approved in advance, and only while you have sharing turned on. This policy explains what the App collects, why, where it is stored, how long it is kept, and how you can delete it.

2. Where information is stored, and third parties

  • Everything except the information that stays on your iPhone is stored and processed by Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore). Google acts as a service provider processing information on our instructions. Data location: Cloud Firestore: Tokyo (asia-northeast1). The App uses the same system as the Android version of "Imadoko Share", so you can also connect with people who use the Android version.
  • To help prevent abuse, the App uses Firebase App Check with Apple's DeviceCheck. Apple's system confirms that a request comes from the genuine App, and the App attaches the resulting token to its requests to Firebase. We do not send your location or messages for this purpose.
  • Your location is obtained through your iPhone's Location Services (Apple's Core Location).
  • Maps are displayed with Apple Maps. When a map is shown, the App receives map data for the area shown from Apple.
  • A relay program set up by us (running on Google Apps Script) deletes from the server, every day, the messages and locations of connections that have ended and invite codes that have expired.
  • After you send a message, to notify the other person's Android device of the new message, the App tells the relay the message identifier, the connection identifier and your sign-in token. The relay checks them against the server and then asks Firebase Cloud Messaging to send a notification to the other person's device. Message text and location are not sent to the relay. The notification carries only the sender's display name and the connection identifier. In this version, iPhones do not receive push notifications.
  • "Sign in with Apple" and "Sign in with Google" are optional (you can also use an email address and password). Apple or Google verifies who you are and passes your email address and an identifier to the authentication service (Firebase Authentication). The App does not ask Apple for your name. The authentication service may keep the name and profile picture URL that Google passes, as part of the sign-in record, but the App does not use them on screen or for other people.
  • Except for the providers above, we do not provide or sell your information to third parties. If we receive a legal request for disclosure, we respond in accordance with the law.
  • The App includes the Firebase and Apple components described above. These components may communicate with Google or Apple as needed for their own operation. We have not been able to verify what they send.
  • Communication is encrypted (TLS). Information from the server is kept only in the App's memory and is not saved to your iPhone's storage. Your routes are excluded from backups such as iCloud.

3. How your location is handled (important)

  • Your location is shared only with approved connections, one person at a time, for whom you have turned on "Share my location with this person". It needs both the other person's approval and your own consent.
  • Before asking for location permission, the App explains on screen what is sent, to whom and when. It first asks for "Allow While Using App", and then, after another explanation screen, for "Always". Even if you do not choose "Always", you can share while the App is running.
  • While the App uses your location when it is closed, the location indicator appears at the top of your iPhone's screen. There is no hidden mode.
  • When you turn sharing off, the other person is told "Sharing off (turned off by them)". When you turn it off you can also remove your last location from their screen.
  • Sharing your location and recording your routes are separate switches that can be turned on and off independently. When both sharing and recording are off, the App does not collect your location.

4. Retention and deletion

  • Location, battery and sharing status: only the latest value is stored and it is overwritten with each update (no history is kept). It is deleted when you turn sharing off and remove your last location, when you end the connection, or when you delete your account. If the other person ends the connection, your last location is deleted the next time you open the App, or by the daily automatic clean-up.
  • Messages: kept until you delete them. You can delete messages you sent at any time, and they disappear from the other person's screen too. When a connection ends, neither of you can read the messages any more, and the messages of that connection are deleted from the server by the daily automatic clean-up.
  • Invite codes: valid for 10 minutes. A used code is deleted when the person who created it approves, declines or ends the connection. Other codes are deleted when the creator cancels them, the next time the creator opens the App, or by the daily automatic clean-up.
  • Routes (on your iPhone): you can keep them for 7 days, 30 days (default), 90 days or forever, and delete them by day or all at once. Signing out deletes the routes saved on this iPhone. Files you export as GPX stay wherever you saved them and cannot be deleted from the App.
  • Deleting your account: in the App, "Settings" > "Delete account" (an Apple or Google account confirms who you are through that sign-in method; for an Apple account, the App's Sign in with Apple access is also revoked) deletes your profile, your sign-in account, your location, the messages you sent and your block list, ends your connections, and removes your display name from them (the other person sees "Deleted user"). The routes on this iPhone are also deleted.
  • Information that may remain: if your iPhone was offline or the App was not opened, messages you sent in a connection that has ended may stay on the server, unreadable by anyone, until the daily automatic clean-up.

5. Your rights

  • You can check and correct your information (you can change your display name in the App) and ask for it to be deleted (see above). If you cannot do something in the App, contact us at yutti0726@gmail.com.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have further rights, such as access, portability or objection. Contact us and we will respond in accordance with the law that applies to you.
  • You can withdraw location and camera permission at any time in the Settings app on your iPhone. Withdrawing location permission stops location sharing.

6. Children

The App is intended for people aged 18 or older. We do not knowingly collect information from younger children. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us at yutti0726@gmail.com and we will delete it.

7. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will tell you on this page and in the App.
