How to Write On and Sign a PDF on Android | No Printing Needed

Updated: October 8, 2026

A contract or consent form arrives as a PDF, and you have to print it out, sign it by hand, and re-scan it before sending it back. If you could handle the signature step entirely on your phone screen, it would save a lot of hassle.

In short, PDF Toolkit's handwritten signature feature lets you sign a document with your finger directly on your phone screen, no printing required, and save or send it as a PDF right away.

Why Is There Growing Demand for Signing PDFs on a Phone?

As more contract and application processes move to email and chat, it's now common for the document itself to arrive as a PDF. But if the signature step alone reverts to a paper workflow — "print, sign, scan" — it becomes a real burden for anyone without a printer or scanner on hand. If it can be handled entirely on a phone, you can sign and send it back on the spot, even while you're out.

How Do I Write On or Sign a PDF in PDF Toolkit on Android?

  1. Open the PDF you want to write on from your library
  2. On the page you want, open the ︙ menu and choose “Sign / write”
  3. Write directly on the screen with your finger (or a stylus). You can pick black, blue or red ink
  4. If you make a mistake, “Undo” removes your last stroke, and “Clear” removes everything so you can start over
  5. Tap “Save” to apply your writing to the page

You can write on one page at a time. The whole page is shown to fit the width of your screen and zooming isn't supported, so small fields can be hard to write in neatly. Also, you can't move or resize what you've drawn, even before saving, so redraw it if it ends up in the wrong place.

When you save, your writing is added to the page as an overlaid image and overwrites the original PDF. You can't remove the writing separately afterward, so if you want to keep the original, make a copy somewhere else before you write.
The signature feature isn't limited to signatures — it also works for free-form handwritten annotations. You can use the same steps to add a checkmark, circle a mistake, or jot a comment in the margin.

How Is This Different from Printing and Signing by Hand?

The biggest difference is that the entire print-and-scan process disappears. With a paper workflow, you'd need to actually write on a printed page by hand and then scan it back into a PDF — signing on your phone means the whole process, start to finish, stays digital. Document exchanges don't grind to a halt just because you're somewhere without a printer or scanner.

Is a Signature Made on a Phone Legally Valid?

This is something that can't be stated as a blanket "yes" or "no," since it depends on the type of document and how the recipient handles it. It's usually not an issue for internal confirmation documents or contracts where the other party already accepts digital signatures, but for contracts where legal validity really matters, it's worth confirming beforehand with the other party or a professional that a digital signature is acceptable. This article covers only how to use the feature and isn't a statement of legal advice.

Summary

Adding a handwritten signature to a PDF lets you skip the print-and-scan process entirely and finish everything right on your phone screen. Since the same feature works for free-form notes as well as signing, it's worth trying if you want to lighten the load of everyday document handling.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q. Can I add a handwritten signature to a PDF on my phone?

A. Yes. PDF Toolkit's handwritten signature feature lets you draw a signature with your finger on-screen and composite it straight onto the PDF for saving.

Q. Can I also add free-form notes, not just a signature?

A. Yes. The same feature works for more than just signing — checkmarks, comments, circling something to highlight it, and other free-form handwritten annotations.

Q. How is this different from printing and signing by hand?

A. It removes the print → sign → re-scan process entirely — you sign right on your phone screen and can send it as-is. The big difference is that it works even without a printer.

Q. Does a signature drawn on a phone look the same as one on paper?

A. Since the lines drawn with your finger or stylus are composited directly onto the page, the same handwriting you'd produce by hand is reflected in the PDF.

Q. Is a signature made this way on a phone legally valid?

A. This depends on the type of document and how the other party handles it, so it can't be stated definitively either way. For important contracts where legal validity matters, it's a good idea to check with the other party or a professional beforehand about whether a digital signature is acceptable.

Q. Can I adjust the position or size of my signature afterward?

A. No. What you draw is saved exactly where you drew it. Before saving, “Undo” removes your last stroke and “Clear” removes everything so you can start over.

Q. Does writing on a PDF overwrite the original?

A. Yes. When you save, your writing is added to the page as an overlaid image and overwrites the original PDF. If you want to keep the original, make a copy somewhere else before you write.

Q. Can I type text with the keyboard?

A. No. You can only add text and lines that you draw with your finger.

Q. Which ink colors can I use?

A. Three: black, blue and red.
