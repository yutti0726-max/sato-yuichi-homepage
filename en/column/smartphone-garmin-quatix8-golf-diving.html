How a Garmin quatix 8 Changed Golf and Diving for Me | Free Virtual Caddie, Free Safety Stops

Updated: September 13, 2026

Switching to an Android-and-Windows setup, I replaced my Apple Watch Ultra with a Garmin quatix 8. As someone who does both golf and diving, here's how much of a difference this one watch made.

In short: yardage measurement and a virtual caddie for golf, and safety-stop tracking and dive logging for diving, all now work with no subscription at all.

Why switch from Apple Watch to Garmin?

The Apple Watch Ultra does have outdoor-focused features, but for something as specialized as golf or diving, I kept running into its limits. The Garmin quatix 8 is built specifically for marine and outdoor use, and it covers both golf and diving to a genuinely practical standard — that's what pulled me over. I cover the full context of this switch in I Used to Be an iPhone Loyalist — Here's Why I Switched to Android + Windows.

What it does for golf

On the course, it shows the remaining distance to the pin automatically. Your scorecard gets tracked for you round after round, with no manual entry. The bigger surprise was the virtual caddie feature, which suggests which club to reach for next — all included, with no separate golf-GPS app subscription required.

What it does for diving

For diving, the safety-stop countdown quietly turns out to be one of the most useful features. Depth, water temperature, and dive time are all logged automatically, covering most of what you'd otherwise need a dedicated dive computer or a separate paid app for.

Use caseWhat it doesCost
GolfYardage · automatic scorecard · virtual caddieNo extra charge
DivingSafety-stop countdown · automatic dive logNo extra charge
Buying a dedicated golf GPS watch and a separate dive computer adds up fast. If you're into both, covering them with one Garmin quatix 8 makes a lot of sense financially too.

Something to keep in mind before buying

The Garmin quatix 8 is a high-end marine and outdoor model, so general online retailers rarely carry the watch itself. If you're considering one, I'd check price, stock, and warranty terms through Garmin's official site or an authorized dealer.

In summary

Switching to a Garmin quatix 8 let me cover two very different hobbies — golf and diving — with a single watch and no subscription. The golf virtual caddie and the dive safety-stop countdown are both features you only really appreciate once you've used them in the field.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q. What can the Garmin quatix 8 do for golf?

A. Yardage to the pin, automatic scorecard tracking, and a virtual caddie that suggests which club to use next — all at no extra cost.

Q. What can it do for diving?

A. It counts down your safety stop for you, and automatically logs depth, water temperature, and dive time — no subscription required.

Q. How does it compare to an Apple Watch?

A. The Apple Watch Ultra has outdoor features too, but the Garmin quatix 8's strength is offering genuinely specialist tools — a golf virtual caddie, dive safety-stop and log tracking — with no extra subscription.

Q. Where can I buy a Garmin quatix 8?

A. It's a high-end marine and outdoor model, so general online retailers rarely stock the watch itself. I'd recommend checking price, stock, and warranty terms through Garmin's official site or an authorized dealer.

Q. Is it worth it if I only golf, or only dive?

A. If you only need one of the two, a cheaper dedicated model might make more sense. The quatix 8 really shines for people who want both covered in a single watch.
