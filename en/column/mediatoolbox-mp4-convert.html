How to Convert a Video to MP4 on Your Phone | Fixing Playback and Upload Errors

Ever tried to open a video shot on your iPhone on a computer, only to find it wouldn't play — or tried to upload a video to a particular web service and got a format error? This article explains how to convert a video to MP4 using only your phone.

Why Videos Won't Play or Upload

By default, videos shot on an iPhone are saved in a format called HEVC (with a .mov extension). HEVC is excellent at keeping file sizes small, but older computers and some web services or business systems don't support it, which causes "won't play" or "won't upload" errors. On Android, too, some models save video in formats with limited playback compatibility.

Most of these problems can be solved by converting the video to the more universal MP4 (H.264) format. MP4 is the de facto standard supported by nearly every computer, phone, and web service.

Ways to Convert a Video to MP4

1. Use video editing/conversion software on a computer

This allows fine-grained settings, but it requires transferring the file to a computer and installing software, so it's not something you can do right away while out and about.

2. Use an online video conversion service

This is convenient since it works entirely in a browser, but it requires uploading your video file to an external server, which raises concerns around privacy and data usage. There's often a file size limit, too.

3. Convert it with a phone app

Just pick a video saved on your phone and convert it to MP4 without uploading it anywhere. The advantage is that everything happens on the spot, with no data usage to worry about.
