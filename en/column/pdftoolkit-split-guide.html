How to Split a PDF on Android | Divide It by Page Count

Updated: October 9, 2026

You want to cut a document of dozens of pages down to a size you can attach to an email, or hand out one file per chapter. You can split a PDF with just your phone, no computer needed. This article explains how to use “Split PDF” in PDF Toolkit and how it differs from “Extract pages.”

In short, enter a number of pages in “Split PDF” and the PDF is divided into several PDFs of that many pages each. When you want to pick out specific pages, use “Extract pages” instead.

When Do You Want to Split a PDF?

  • It is too heavy to send in one message: you want to send a document of dozens of pages across several emails
  • You want to hand out one part per person: a long document divided into a file for each person in charge
  • You want one file per page: receipts, application forms and other documents that must be submitted one sheet at a time

How Do I Split a PDF?

  1. In PDF Toolkit, open “Tools” (the wrench icon) at the top right of the Library screen
  2. Tap “Split PDF” and pick the PDF you want to split
  3. Under “Pages per file,” enter how many pages you want in each file, then tap the confirm button
  4. The split documents, such as “Split PDF 1” and “Split PDF 2,” are added to your library

Your original PDF stays as it is. For example, entering “10” for a 30-page PDF gives three PDFs of 10 pages each. If it does not divide evenly, only the last file has fewer pages. Entering “1” splits it into one-page PDFs.

Splitting divides evenly by “Pages per file.” You cannot split by a range such as “pages 3 to 5 only.” When you want specific pages, use “Extract pages,” described next.

How Do I Choose Between “Split” and “Extract Pages”?

What you wantFeature to useWhat you get
A long document in several files of the same page countSplit PDFSeveral PDFs with the page count you set
One PDF per pageSplit PDF (set Pages per file to 1)As many PDFs as there are pages
Only pages 3 to 5, or specific pages that are not next to each otherExtract pagesOne PDF with just the pages you pick

The steps for “Extract pages” are in How to Rotate and Extract PDF Pages.

What Is Useful After Splitting?

  • If the files are still heavy: “Compress PDF” under Tools can make them smaller, but image quality drops and text can no longer be searched or copied. See PDF Too Big to Send? Compress, Split or Extract Pages for details
  • If you want to put them back together: “Merge PDFs” under Tools combines two or more split PDFs into one again

On the free version, PDFs you share or send carry a small “Created with PDF Toolkit” at the bottom of each page. To remove it, use “Export without watermark” (watching one video ad covers that PDF once) or the PRO version (one-time purchase).

Summary

You can split a PDF into several files on your phone just by entering a number under “Pages per file.” Your original PDF stays, so you can try it as often as you like. When you want to pull out specific pages, choose “Extract pages” rather than “Split.”

Frequently Asked Questions

Q. What does it mean to split a PDF?

A. It means dividing one PDF into several PDF files of a set number of pages each.

Q. Does my original PDF remain after splitting?

A. Yes. The results are added to your library as new documents such as “Split PDF 1” and “Split PDF 2,” and your original PDF stays as it is.

Q. Can I make a separate PDF for each page?

A. Yes. Enter 1 under “Pages per file” and the PDF is split into one-page PDFs.

Q. Can I split by a range such as pages 3 to 5 only?

A. Not with Split, which divides evenly by page count. When you want specific pages, use “Extract pages” under Tools and check the pages you need.

Q. Can I put the split PDFs back together later?

A. Yes. “Merge PDFs” under Tools combines two or more split PDFs into one.
