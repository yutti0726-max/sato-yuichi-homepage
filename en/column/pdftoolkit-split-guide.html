How to Split a PDF for Free | Computer or Android

Updated: November 20, 2026

You want to split a PDF but are not sure which method suits you. This article covers free ways to do it on a computer and on an Android phone. On a computer, you use features already built into your browser or Mac; on an Android phone, you use the free app “PDF Toolkit.”

In short, on a computer, open the PDF in your browser (Chrome or Edge), choose the save-as-PDF option in the print screen and set a page range, and you get just those pages as a new PDF. On a Mac, Preview can pull pages out too. On an Android phone, “Split PDF” in PDF Toolkit divides a PDF into files of a page count you choose.

When Do You Want to Split a PDF?

  • It is too heavy to send in one message: you want to send a document of dozens of pages across several emails
  • You want to hand out one part per person: a long document divided into a file for each person in charge
  • You want one file per page: receipts, application forms and other documents that must be submitted one sheet at a time

How Do I Split a PDF on a Computer? (Free, Built-In Features)

Chrome or Edge: print and save as PDF

  1. Open the PDF you want to split in your browser (Chrome or Edge)
  2. Open the print screen (Ctrl+P, or ⌘+P on a Mac)
  3. Set the destination (printer) to “Save as PDF” or a similar option that saves as a PDF
  4. Change the pages setting from “All” to a range, and enter the pages you want, such as “3-5”
  5. Click Save and save it under a different file name from the original

Repeat with a different range to get several files. For a 30-page PDF, for example, that is three rounds: “1-10,” “11-20” and “21-30.” If you save under a different file name, your original PDF stays as it is.

Mac: pull pages out with Preview

  1. Open the PDF in Preview and show the thumbnails (the list of small page images) from the View menu
  2. Select the thumbnails of the pages you want (hold the ⌘ key to select more than one)
  3. Drag the selected thumbnails to the desktop or another folder

The selected pages become a new PDF file. Menu names and steps can differ a little depending on your macOS version.

How Do I Split a PDF on an Android Phone?

Use “Split PDF” in PDF Toolkit.

  1. In PDF Toolkit, open “Tools” (the wrench icon) at the top right of the Library screen
  2. Tap “Split PDF” and pick the PDF you want to split
  3. Under “Pages per file,” enter how many pages you want in each file, then tap the confirm button
  4. The split documents, such as “Split PDF 1” and “Split PDF 2,” are added to your library

Your original PDF stays as it is. For example, entering “10” for a 30-page PDF gives three PDFs of 10 pages each. If it does not divide evenly, only the last file has fewer pages. Entering “1” splits it into one-page PDFs.

Splitting divides evenly by “Pages per file.” You cannot split by a range such as “pages 3 to 5 only.” When you want specific pages, use “Extract pages,” described next.

What Is Useful After Splitting?

  • If the files are still heavy: “Compress PDF” under Tools in PDF Toolkit can make them smaller, but image quality drops and text can no longer be searched or copied. See PDF Too Big to Send? Compress, Split or Extract Pages for details
  • If you want to put them back together: “Merge PDFs” under Tools in PDF Toolkit combines two or more split PDFs into one again

On the free version of PDF Toolkit, PDFs you share or send carry a small “Created with PDF Toolkit” at the bottom of each page. To remove it, use “Export without watermark” (watching one video ad covers that PDF once) or the PRO version (one-time purchase).

Summary

On a computer, you can split a PDF for free by printing from your browser and saving as PDF with a page range, or with Preview on a Mac. On an Android phone, PDF Toolkit divides a PDF into several files by “Pages per file,” and your original PDF stays. When you want to pull out specific pages, choose “Extract pages” rather than “Split.”

Frequently Asked Questions

Q. Is there a free way to split a PDF?

A. Yes. On a computer, you can print from your browser (Chrome or Edge), choose a save-as-PDF option and set a page range, or use Preview on a Mac to pull pages out; both are built-in features. On an Android phone, the free app PDF Toolkit has “Split PDF,” which divides a PDF by page count. Screen names can differ slightly depending on your version.

Q. How can I pull out just pages 3 to 5 on a computer?

A. Open the PDF in your browser, open the print screen, set the destination to save as PDF, enter a page range such as 3-5, and save. On a Mac, you can also select those pages’ thumbnails in Preview and drag them to the desktop or a folder.

Q. Can I make a separate PDF for each page on my phone?

A. With PDF Toolkit on Android, enter 1 under “Pages per file” in “Split PDF” and the PDF is split into one-page PDFs.

Q. Can I split by a range such as pages 3 to 5 only on my phone?

A. Not with “Split PDF” in PDF Toolkit, which divides evenly by page count. When you want specific pages, use “Extract pages” under Tools and check the pages you need.

Q. Does my original PDF remain after splitting?

A. In PDF Toolkit, yes. The results are added to your library as new documents such as “Split PDF 1” and “Split PDF 2,” and your original PDF stays as it is. With the print-to-PDF method on a computer, your original also stays as long as you save the new file under a different name.

Q. Can I put the split PDFs back together later?

A. In PDF Toolkit, “Merge PDFs” under Tools combines two or more split PDFs into one.
