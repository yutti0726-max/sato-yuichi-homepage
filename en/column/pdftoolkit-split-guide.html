How to Split a PDF | Extract Just the Pages You Need on Your Phone

Updated: September 7, 2026

You want to send just a few pages from a document that's dozens of pages long. Rather than sending the whole PDF, it's handy to be able to pull out just the range you need. This article explains how to split a PDF using only a phone app.

In short, specifying a page range in the free PDF Toolkit app is all it takes to pull just the pages you need out of a large PDF as a new file.

When Would I Want to Split a PDF?

Say a government office or business partner sends you a document dozens of pages long, but only a handful of pages actually concern you and need to go to someone else. Or you need to pull out one specific clause from a contract for someone to review. In situations like these, cutting out just the range you need — rather than forwarding the whole PDF — also reduces the burden on whoever receives it.

How Do I Split a PDF?

Dedicated computer software can split PDFs too, but that requires transferring the file to a computer first, which isn't quick when you're out and about. With a phone app, you can specify the page range on the spot and export just the part you need as a new PDF.

How Do I Split a PDF in PDF Toolkit?

  1. In PDF Toolkit, open the PDF you want to split
  2. From the menu, select "Split PDF"
  3. Specify the page numbers or range you want to extract
  4. The specified range is exported as a new PDF file

The original PDF stays untouched, so you can cut out the range you need as a separate file as many times as you like.

It helps to check the page numbers you need beforehand. For a document with a lot of pages, quickly reviewing the table of contents or page numbers before you start makes it much easier to avoid specifying the wrong range.

What Pairs Well with Splitting?

If a split-off PDF is still too large, "Compress PDF" can shrink it further. Conversely, if you want to reorganize several split PDFs back into one, "Merge PDFs" lets you combine them again. Split, merge, and compress work together so you can handle all your PDF organizing in one app.

Summary

Splitting a PDF is handy whenever you want to share just part of a larger document. With a phone app, you can create a new PDF on the spot just by specifying a page range, keeping document exchanges smooth without ever touching a computer.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q. What does splitting a PDF actually do?

A. It divides one multi-page PDF into multiple PDF files, cut along the page ranges you specify.

Q. What happens to the original PDF after splitting?

A. The original PDF stays untouched — the range you specify is exported separately as a new PDF.

Q. Can I split it into individual PDFs, one page at a time?

A. Yes. Specify the page range as one page at a time, and each page will be exported as its own individual PDF.

Q. Can split PDFs be merged back into one?

A. Yes. Using the "Merge PDFs" feature, split PDFs (or any other PDFs) can be combined back into one file.

Q. When would I actually use splitting?

A. It's handy when you want to share just the pages you need from a large document, or submit only part of a contract.
