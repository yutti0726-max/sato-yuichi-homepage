In short, the free app MediaToolbox lets you freely convert an image's format between JPEG, PNG, and WEBP, solving "won't open" or "too heavy" problems entirely on your phone.

How Are JPEG, PNG, and WEBP Different?

FormatCharacteristicsBest suited for
JPEGHigh compression, small file size — but quality degrades with repeated savesSaving and sharing photos
PNGLossless compression preserves quality; supports transparencyLogos, illustrations, screenshots
WEBPSmall and high-quality thanks to strong compression; a relatively new format not supported everywhereDisplaying images on websites

When Would I Need to Convert an Image's Format?

  • A WEBP image won't open: older software or some systems don't support WEBP
  • A PNG image is too heavy: converting a photo-like PNG to JPEG shrinks the file size
  • I want to use it as a transparent image: converting JPEG to PNG lets you use transparency
  • I need to match a required format: an application form or similar requires a specific image format

Frequently Asked Questions

Q. What's the difference between JPEG, PNG, and WEBP?

A. JPEG is suited to photos and keeps file size small; PNG preserves transparency and sharp text edges; WEBP offers high compression with a small file size while keeping image quality high.

Q. Why won't a WEBP image open?

A. WEBP is a relatively new format, so some older software or systems don't support it. Converting it to JPEG or PNG may let it open.

Q. Does converting an image reduce its quality?

A. Converting to a compressed format like JPEG can cause some quality loss, but a lossless format like PNG preserves quality almost entirely.

Q. Is the capture date and time preserved when converting?

A. Yes. MediaToolbox's conversion preserves metadata (EXIF information) like capture date and time, so you can convert just the format without disrupting your photo organization.

Q. Can I convert multiple images at once?

A. Yes, selecting multiple images when choosing which to convert lets you convert them all to the same format at once.
