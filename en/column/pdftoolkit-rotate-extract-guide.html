How to Rotate and Extract PDF Pages | From Fixing Orientation to Pulling Out Just the Pages You Need

Updated: September 7, 2026

One page of a scanned PDF came out sideways, or you just want to pull a handful of pages out of a large document to send to someone. These small, page-level adjustments can also be done entirely with a phone app.

In short, PDF Toolkit's rotate feature fixes a page's orientation individually, and its extract feature pulls out just the pages you need as a new PDF.

Why Does a PDF's Page Orientation End Up Wrong?

When you photograph a paper document with your phone's camera to turn it into a PDF, if the orientation of the document or the phone (portrait vs. landscape) varies from page to page, the resulting PDF can end up with inconsistent page orientations. That means tilting your head every time you view or print it — fixing the orientation makes it much more comfortable to work with.

How Do I Rotate a Page in PDF Toolkit?

  1. Open the PDF whose orientation you want to fix and view the page list
  2. Open the menu from the thumbnail of the page you want to rotate
  3. Select Rotate and turn it 90 degrees at a time until it's oriented correctly
  4. Save the change

Since each page can be rotated individually, if only some pages in a PDF are oriented wrong, you can fix just those pages precisely.

How Do I Extract Just the Pages I Need?

  1. Open the source PDF
  2. Select the page extraction feature
  3. Check the pages you want to pull out
  4. Save just the pages you selected as a single new PDF

Since the original PDF is left untouched, you can safely pull out part of a large document as a separate file without disturbing the original data.

"Extract" and "split" sound similar but serve different purposes. Extract lets you pick out just the pages you need, even ones that aren't next to each other, and combine them into one file. Split cuts the file itself into multiple pieces along continuous page ranges. See also How to Split a PDF.

Can I Also Export a Page as an Image?

If you want to share just one specific page on social media or chat, you can also export that page as an image. Select the page from the page list, export it as an image, and it's ready to send or post in JPEG or PNG format. Compared to sending an entire PDF file, this makes it easy to share content regardless of the viewer's environment.

When Would I Actually Use This?

  • Some pages of a scanned document came out sideways: fix them with page rotation
  • I want to send just the necessary clause from a contract: pull it out with page extraction
  • I want to post one page of a document on social media: share it as an image

Summary

Inconsistent page orientation, or needing to pull out just certain pages, are both common needs when handling documents. PDF Toolkit lets you rotate, extract, and share pages as images all in one app, with no need to transfer files back to a computer.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q. Why does a scanned PDF's page sometimes come out sideways?

A. The orientation you held your phone in while photographing a paper document (portrait or landscape) can leave page orientations inconsistent once it's turned into a PDF. The rotate feature lets you fix each page's orientation individually.

Q. Can I rotate pages individually?

A. Yes. You can specify rotation for each page individually rather than the whole PDF at once, so even if several pages are oriented differently, you can fix each one correctly.

Q. What's the difference between "extract" and "split"?

A. Extract lets you pick just the pages you need — even ones that aren't next to each other — and combine them into one new PDF. Split cuts a file into multiple PDFs along continuous page ranges. If you want to pull pages 1–3 and page 8 together into one file, use extract; if you want to split off pages 1–3 as one file and everything from page 4 onward as another, use split.

Q. Does the original PDF still exist after extracting?

A. Yes. Extracting creates a new PDF from the original, so the original file is left untouched.

Q. Can I export just one specific page as an image?

A. Yes. Choosing "Share as image" on any page exports just that page as a JPEG, PNG, or similar image, which you can then share on social media or chat apps.

Q. Does the file size change after rotating or extracting?

A. Extracting reduces the page count, so the resulting file is smaller than the original PDF. Rotating only changes each page's orientation data, so it has essentially no effect on file size.
