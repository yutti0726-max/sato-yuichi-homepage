A Complete Home-Office Desk Setup | Choosing a Keyboard, Monitor, Mouse and Numpad

Updated: October 3, 2026

Working from home, you spend most of the day at the same desk with the same keyboard and mouse. The input devices and screens your hands and eyes touch every day affect how tired you get far more than the computer's specs do. Here is a complete home-office desk setup, based on the gear I actually use.

In short: the most effective way to set up a home-office desk is to start with three things — a mouse and keyboard that fit your hands, an external monitor you can place at eye level, and a wrist rest to support your wrists.

Why I chose each item

Keyboard: Keychron Q3 Ultra 8K

A tenkeyless mechanical keyboard. I swapped in silent switches so the typing noise doesn't bother anyone even late at night (the swap, step by step).

Numpad: EPOMAKER EK21 VIA

The keyboard itself is tenkeyless, and the numpad sits beside it only when I need to enter numbers. That keeps the mouse close to my body, so my shoulders don't open up too far and I tire less.

Mice: ATK FIERCE X and MX ANYWHERE 3S

With mice, fit matters more than specs. My hands are small and I use a claw grip, so I use the ATK FIERCE X at the desk and the MX ANYWHERE 3S on the go (how I choose).

Monitors: Titan Army P245MS+ and EVICIV portable monitor

A 24.5" WQHD monitor as the main screen and a portable monitor as the second makes a two-screen setup. Writing while reading reference material, or keeping a meeting window open to the side, gets much easier when working from home.

Desk extender and wrist rest

A desk extender adds depth without replacing the desk, and a wooden wrist rest supports the wrists. Together they reduce the strain of long typing sessions.

Notes on setup and maintenance

  • Placing the top of the monitor at or slightly below eye level makes your neck and shoulders tire less.
  • Even with silent switches, the sound depends on how you type. If noise matters where you work, a desk mat cuts the echo.
  • More cables mean more dust. Clean under the desk and around the computer regularly.
