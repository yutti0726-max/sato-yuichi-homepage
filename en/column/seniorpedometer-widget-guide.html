How to Add a Pedometer Widget to Your Home Screen | Check Your Steps at a Glance

Updated: September 7, 2026

Opening a pedometer app every single time can feel like a chore. Add a widget that shows your step count on the home screen, and you'll know today's total the moment you glance at your phone.

In short, long-press your home screen, choose "Widgets," then pick Rakuraku Step Counter to place a widget showing today's steps and calories in large text — freely resizable to whatever size suits you.

What Does Adding a Widget Actually Get You?

A widget is a feature that displays information directly on your home screen without needing to open the app. With a pedometer app, that means today's steps and calories show up every time you glance at your phone, with no need to launch the app just to check. It's especially handy if you're not used to app navigation, or if you like to check your progress often throughout the day.

How Do I Add the Widget to My Home Screen?

  1. Long-press an empty area of your home screen
  2. From the menu that appears, choose "Widgets"
  3. Find "Rakuraku Step Counter" in the widget list
  4. Long-press the widget and drag it to the spot you want on your home screen
  5. Long-press the placed widget's frame and drag the handles at the edges to a size that's easy to read

The exact screens differ a bit depending on your Android version and home-screen launcher, but the "long-press home screen → Widgets" flow is common to nearly every device.

If the text looks too small to read comfortably, try resizing the widget larger. Text scales up automatically as you enlarge it, which helps a lot if your eyes tire easily.

How Much Can the Widget Be Resized?

The Rakuraku Step Counter widget can be adjusted anywhere from its minimum size up to full size. When you have room on your home screen, make it large for an at-a-glance view; if you want to fit it alongside other app icons or widgets, shrink it down.

What Can I Check with the Widget Alone?

The widget shows that day's steps and calories. For more detail — like your title rank as your walking distance climbs, or your history of past days — tap the widget or open the app itself. For a simple daily check, the widget alone is all you need.

Summary

Setting up a home-screen widget means today's step count naturally catches your eye every time you check your phone, without the extra step of opening the app. Since the text size is adjustable too, set it up at whatever size is easiest for you to read.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q. What is a home-screen widget?

A. It's a small window that displays information directly on your home screen without needing to open the app. Rakuraku Step Counter's widget shows today's steps and calories in large text.

Q. Can I change the widget's text size?

A. Yes. Making the widget bigger automatically makes the text bigger too. If your eyes tire easily, a larger size is recommended.

Q. Do I still need to open the app once I add the widget?

A. For a daily glance at your step count, the widget alone is enough. Open the app only when you want more detail, like your title rank or past-day history.

Q. Sometimes the widget doesn't seem to update

A. It's designed to update in real time as you walk, but your device's battery-saving settings can occasionally delay updates. Excluding Rakuraku Step Counter from battery optimization keeps it stable.

Q. Can the widget also be made small?

A. Yes. It can be shrunk down to a minimum size, which also works well if you want to keep your home screen tidy alongside other widgets.

Q. What if I accidentally remove the widget?

A. Removing the widget doesn't delete the app or any of your recorded history. Add it again using the same steps and your steps will keep displaying as before.
