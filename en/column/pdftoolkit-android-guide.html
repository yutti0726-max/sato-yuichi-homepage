How to Edit a PDF on Your Phone (Android): What You Can and Can't Do, and a Free App

Updated: October 8, 2026

"I want to merge PDFs." "I want to delete a page." "I want to shrink a PDF that's too heavy to send." "I need to sign this." A lot of PDF editing on Android can be done with a free app, no computer required. Rewriting the text that's already in a PDF, though, is hard to do on a phone. This article separates what you can and can't edit on your phone, then walks through the PDF editing app "PDF Toolkit" feature by feature.

In short: merging, splitting, reordering, deleting and rotating pages, compressing the file, OCR, and handwritten signatures or notes all work on your phone with the free app "PDF Toolkit". Rewriting text that's already in the PDF doesn't — the reliable fix is to correct the original file (such as a Word document) and create the PDF again.

What Are My Options for Editing PDFs on Android?

There are broadly three ways to edit PDFs on Android: transferring files to a computer and using software there, using a browser-based online service, or editing directly with a phone app. Transferring to a computer is a hassle, and online services leave you uneasy about uploading documents that may contain personal information. A phone app lets you finish the job on the spot, wherever you are, without sensitive documents ever leaving your device.

A PDF is closer to a printed page than to an editable document, so it isn't designed for rewriting the original text. When the wording needs to change, the most reliable route is to fix the file the PDF was made from and save it as a PDF again. If you don't have the original, or the fix is small, you can also write the correction in by hand.

When Would I Use Each Feature?

  • I have a paper document on hand: Scan to PDF
  • I have documents I've already photographed: Create PDF from photos
  • I want to combine submissions into one file: Merge PDFs
  • It's too heavy to send by email or chat: Compress PDFs
  • I want to share just part of a large document: Extract pages
  • I want to search or copy text later: OCR text recognition
  • A contract needs a signature: Handwritten signature & notes
Handling everything in one app saves you the trouble of learning multiple apps and shuttling documents back and forth between them. An app with OCR support in particular makes a big difference to how searchable your documents stay afterward — worth weighing when choosing.

What Should I Look For When Choosing an App?

  • Whether it's free to use (ads, watermarks, and what the paid version adds)
  • Whether scan, merge, split, compress, and OCR are all handled in one app
  • Whether it supports your language
  • Whether processing happens entirely on-device, without sending documents to an external server

Frequently Asked Questions

Q. Do I need dedicated computer software to edit PDFs on Android?

A. No. With an app like PDF Toolkit, you can merge, split, compress, OCR, and sign PDFs — all from your phone.

Q. Is there a free app for editing PDFs?

A. Yes. Every PDF Toolkit feature is free to use, but PDFs exported on the free plan carry a small watermark. Watching one short video ad lets you share that PDF without the watermark, and the one-time PRO purchase removes both the ads and the watermark.

Q. Can I turn a paper document directly into a PDF?

A. Yes. The "Scan to PDF" feature converts a photo you take with your camera into a deskewed, cropped PDF.

Q. What should I look for when choosing a PDF editing app?

A. Whether it's free to use, whether scan, merge, split, and compress are all handled in one app, whether it supports OCR, and whether it supports your language are the key points.

Q. Can I make text in a scanned PDF searchable and copyable?

A. Using OCR, scanned text can be recognized and turned into a state that's searchable and copyable.

Q. Can I add a handwritten signature to a PDF?

A. Yes. It supports adding a handwritten signature or notes directly onto the document.

Q. Can I change the existing text in a PDF on my phone?

A. Not with PDF Toolkit — it can't rewrite text that's already in the PDF. The reliable fix is to correct the original file (Word, Google Docs and so on) and export a new PDF. For a small correction, you can also write it in by hand with the signature and notes tool.

Q. Can I reorder pages or delete pages I don't need?

A. Yes. From a document's page-order screen you can rearrange pages and remove the ones you don't need.

Q. Can I fill in PDF form fields on my phone?

A. PDF Toolkit has no feature for typing into a PDF's form fields with the keyboard. Instead, “Sign / write” lets you write directly on top of a blank field with your finger. Ink comes in three colors (black, blue and red), and saving overwrites the original PDF, so it is safest to keep a separate copy of the original before you write.

Q. Can I erase what I wrote on a PDF afterward?

A. Before saving, “Undo” removes your last stroke and “Clear” removes everything you wrote. Once you save, the writing is merged into the page as an image and overwrites the original PDF, so you can not remove it separately afterward.
