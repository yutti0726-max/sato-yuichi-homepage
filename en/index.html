MediaToolbox

A simple multimedia conversion tool that handles video, images, audio, and PDFs all in one place.

Free 🌐 Japanese / English
  • [Resize Video] Convert to MP4 at your choice of resolution — 1080p, 720p, 360p, and more
  • [Resize Image] Freely set width and height, with an optional aspect-ratio lock
  • [Extract Audio] Pull just the audio track out of a video and save it as M4A
  • [PDF to Images] Export every page of a PDF as JPEG or PNG
  • [Images to PDF] Build a PDF from photos taken with the camera or already saved on your phone

PDF Toolkit

Scan, merge, split, compress, and recognize text with OCR — the everyday PDF tasks, all in one app.

Free 🌐 Japanese / English
  • [Scan to PDF] Photograph a document and it's auto-corrected into a PDF
  • [Images to PDF] Combine multiple photos into a single PDF
  • [Merge PDFs] Combine multiple PDF files into one
  • [Split PDF] Split a PDF by page range or interval
  • [Compress PDF] Shrink file size (pages are converted to images)
  • [OCR Text Recognition] Make scanned text searchable and copyable
  • [Rotate Pages] Fix pages that are facing the wrong way
  • [Extract Pages] Pull out just the pages you need into a new PDF
  • [Share Page as Image] Share a single PDF page as a JPEG or PNG
  • [Handwritten Notes] Add a signature or note directly on the PDF

Rakuraku Step Counter

No buttons to press — automatic step tracking designed to be easy for seniors.

Free 🌐 Japanese / English
  • [Automatic Step Counting] Tracks your steps in the background — no need to even open the app
  • [Home-Screen Widget] Shows today's steps and calories in large, easy-to-read text, resizable to fit your screen
  • [Rank System] Your rank climbs with the distance you've walked — a game-like journey from Tokyo toward conquering the length of Japan
  • [Daily Goal Alerts] Get notified on hitting 3,000 / 5,000 / 8,000 / 10,000 / 15,000 steps in a day

Readshot

Just take a screenshot and it automatically reads the text — a screenshot manager with built-in OCR.

Free 🌐 Japanese / English
  • [Auto-Detect + Instant OCR] The moment you take a screenshot, it's detected automatically and text is recognized (OCR) right on the device — the result pops up immediately
  • [Copy or Share Just What You Need] Drag to select just part of the recognized text to copy or share (copies the full text if nothing is selected)
  • [URLs Open With a Tap] Any URL found in the recognized text is automatically turned into a link you can tap to open in your browser
  • [History List] Look back at the recognition results from past screenshots in a list
  • [Delete Individually or Clean Up in Bulk] Delete the history entry and the screenshot image itself together, or clean up clutter all at once
  • [Memo Feature] A notepad for jotting down text or pasting in copied text to save (URLs inside are tappable too)
  • [On-Device Processing for Privacy] Text recognition runs entirely on the device — images and recognized text are never sent to an external server
  • [Built-In Guide] A how-to page is always one tap away from the button in the top right
📲 Free Download

Profile

Yuichi Sato is an entrepreneur, application developer, and business consultant based in Hokkaido, Japan. He built his career in telecom infrastructure sales — door-to-door fiber-optic internet (Flet's Hikari, au Hikari, TEPCO Hikari) and business phone sales — before moving into advanced insurance consulting: individual life and third-sector (medical, cancer) insurance at Japan Post Insurance, auto and other property insurance at Tokio Marine Nichido, and corporate life insurance solutions. Across this wide range of financial and insurance products, he has systematized an approach to reading customer psychology and closing deals.

Career & Achievements

Led store operations and sales strategy as a store manager and area supervisor for mobile carrier shops.
  • Insurance & Financial Consulting Proposed life and third-sector insurance at Japan Post Insurance, handled auto and other property insurance at Tokio Marine Nichido, and led corporate life insurance sales. Worked solution-based sales addressing complex needs, from individual life planning to corporate financial strategy.
  • Technology & Development Drawing on experience building over 100 custom PCs and deep hardware/software knowledge, he plans and develops practical applications that turn users' latent needs into real products.

By combining the ability to identify customers' underlying problems — honed on the front lines — with the hardware and software insight gained from building and testing 100+ custom PCs and gadgets, he plans, builds, and ships applications that are both genuinely useful and distinctly his own.
