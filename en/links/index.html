SIGN is the brand of Yuichi Sato, an independent developer who makes watch faces and apps for Garmin watches, Android apps and Kindle books.

Garmin watch faces and apps

Watch faces, data fields and apps that work on Garmin watches. They are not Garmin products: I develop them independently and publish them on the Garmin Connect IQ Store. Payment for the paid editions is handled by the third-party service KiezelPay, not Garmin.

  • AurumA frameless gold watch face built like a fine watch dial, with six finishesPaid, one-time purchase (24h free trial)
  • PlatinaA platinum-tone luxury watch face on guilloche-style dials, with five finishes and three layouts for 15 looksPaid, one-time purchase (48h free trial)
  • EmpressA frameless jewelled chronograph watch face with five gemstone finishesPaid, one-time purchase (24h free trial)
  • OverwatchA watch face with five data layouts that switch automaticallyPaid, one-time purchase (24h free trial)
  • KichijitsuA Japanese lucky-day watch face (rokuyo, Ichiryu Manbai-bi, Tenshanichi), plus the Kichijitsu Calendar app that lists upcoming lucky daysKichijitsu: paid, one-time purchase (24h free trial) / Kichijitsu Calendar: free
  • CairnA hiking data field built for the climbFree edition available / Cairn Pro: paid, one-time purchase (5-activity free trial)
  • Cairn CycleA cycling data field built for speed, power and cadenceFree edition available / Cairn Cycle Pro: paid, one-time purchase (5-activity free trial)
  • MultitimeA multi-timer app that runs several named timers at onceFree edition available (up to 3 timers) / Multitime Pro: paid, one-time purchase
