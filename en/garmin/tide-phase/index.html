Tide Phase | A Fishing Watch Face for Garmin with Japanese Tide Periods and Moon Age

Updated: October 30, 2026

Tide Phase is a fishing watch face that shows today's traditional Japanese tide period (Spring, Mid, Neap, Long or Young tide) and the moon age on your wrist. It comes in two versions: the paid Tide Phase, which adds sunrise and sunset and a 7-day tide period strip, and the free, simpler Tide Phase Lite, which shows today's tide period in large text.

In short: the tide period is a general guide based on the lunar calendar date. It does not show tide times or tide heights. If today's tide period and moon age are all you need, the free Lite is enough; if you also want sunrise and sunset and the tide periods for today and the next 6 days, try the paid version (48-hour free trial, then a one-time US$3.99).

How the tide period is decided

By the day of the lunar month (traditional Japanese tide periods):

Tide periodDays of the lunar month
Spring tide (Oshio)1–3, 14–17, 29–30
Mid tide (Nakashio)4–7, 12–13, 18–21, 27–28
Neap tide (Koshio)8–9, 22–24
Long tide (Nagashio)10 and 25
Young tide (Wakashio)11 and 26

About the tide period and moon age calculation

  • The tide period is a general guide based on the lunar calendar date. Tide Phase does not show tide times or tide heights (no high or low water times). Please check a tide table or other source for those.
  • Actual tides at your location can differ from the calendar-based tide period. Do not rely on it for navigation or other safety decisions, such as whether it is safe to go out fishing.
  • The lunar calendar is based on Japan Standard Time. Outside Japan, the day count can differ by one day from a calendar based on your local time.
  • The tide period and moon age are calculated on the watch from a built-in table of new moon times (2025–2036, from astronomical calculation). No connection is needed for the tide period and moon age.
  • The moon age is the value at the moment it is shown. It can differ by up to about half a day (0.5) from the noon moon age often used in tide tables.
  • The calendar data runs until December 31, 2036. After that, the face shows "Tide data has ended".

Price and how to buy

The paid Tide Phase is a one-time-purchase watch face. Every feature is free to try for 48 hours after you install it. When the trial ends, a single one-time unlock (US$3.99, no subscription) keeps all features working.

Once the trial is over, the dial shows a purchase code and the URL www.kzl.io/code until the face is bought. Open that URL on your phone or computer, enter the code and pay US$3.99 once, and the face unlocks automatically.

Payment is processed by the third-party service KiezelPay, not by Garmin. The purchase screen on the watch is in English.

Tide Phase Lite is free, with no trial or purchase screen. The paid version is not available for the three black-and-white screen watches; please use Lite on those models.

The paid Tide Phase uses the network only for KiezelPay's trial and purchase check, in the background. Sunrise and sunset are calculated from a position the watch already has (the weather observation location, or the most recent position); the face does not request the location permission. Without a position, they show "--:--". The watch faces' own code does not send your location, steps or heart rate anywhere. Lite uses no permissions and makes no network connections of its own.
