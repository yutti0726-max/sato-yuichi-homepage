Cairn Cycle | A Garmin Data Field Built for Speed and Power

Updated: September 25, 2026

Cairn Cycle is a full-screen cycling data field, built as the cycling edition of the hiking-focused data field Cairn. The free edition shows six metrics; the paid Cairn Cycle Pro expands to twelve, all on one screen.

Short version: the free edition alone gives you six ride-focused metrics — speed, distance, heart rate, power, cadence — and Cairn Cycle Pro adds climb data when you want it.

Cairn Cycle / Cairn Cycle Pro

One screen, only the numbers that matter on a ride — nothing to scroll for. Every cell drawn the same way — small label, big value — so you read it at a glance.

Price and how to buy

The free Cairn Cycle needs nothing beyond installing it — all six metrics are free forever, with no network access and no permissions requested.

Cairn Cycle Pro is listed free but is the paid edition. The first 5 activities you use it in are a free trial with every feature working. After that the field shows a 6-digit code and the URL www.kzl.io/code until you unlock it. A one-time payment of US$2.49 (no subscription) unlocks all twelve metrics for good. Open www.kzl.io/code on your phone, enter the code and pay, and it unlocks within a few minutes. Payment is processed by KiezelPay (card or PayPal), not Garmin. Other watches on the same Garmin account don't need a second purchase.

The free Cairn Cycle never connects to anything and requests no permissions. Cairn Cycle Pro only talks to the KiezelPay server to check unlock status, and never sends speed, power, location or any other data anywhere.

FAQ

Q. Is Cairn Cycle free to use?

A. Yes. The free Cairn Cycle is a six-metric cycling data field and is completely free, with no network access and no permissions requested.

Q. What does Cairn Cycle Pro add?

A. Cairn Cycle Pro expands to twelve metrics, adding average speed, average power, elevation, grade, temperature and watch battery. The first 5 activities are a free trial; after that a one-time US$2.49 payment unlocks it for good.

Q. Where does Cairn Cycle Pro's payment go?

A. Payment is processed by the third-party service KiezelPay, not Garmin directly. Once the trial runs out, the field shows a 6-digit code and a URL — open it on your phone, enter the code and pay (credit card or PayPal).

Q. Can I use it without a power meter?

A. Yes. The POWER and CADENCE cells show "--" without a power meter or cadence sensor, but every other cell (speed, distance, time, heart rate, etc.) works normally.

Q. How is this different from the hiking Cairn?

A. Cairn Cycle uses a metric set tuned for cycling (speed, power, cadence, etc.) and is listed separately on the Garmin store from the hiking-focused Cairn. The underlying engine is shared, but the supported activity type and displayed metrics differ.
