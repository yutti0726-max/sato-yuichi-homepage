Price and how to buy

Everything is free to use for 24 hours after you install it. When the trial ends, a single one-time unlock (US$5.99, no subscription) keeps all features working.

Once the trial is over, the dial shows a 6-digit code and the URL www.kzl.io/code. Open that URL on your phone, enter the code and pay, and the watch face unlocks automatically within a few minutes. Payment is processed by KiezelPay, not by Garmin (credit card or PayPal). Once you've unlocked it, other supported watches on the same Garmin account don't need a second purchase.

Aurum never sends step, heart-rate, or any other personal data anywhere. It only talks to the KiezelPay server, to check the unlock status (in the background, roughly once every few minutes).

FAQ

Q. Is Aurum free to use?

A. Everything is free to use for 24 hours after you install it. When the trial ends, a single one-time unlock (US$5.99) keeps all features working. There is no subscription.

Q. Where does payment happen?

A. Payment is processed by the third-party service KiezelPay, not by Garmin directly. Once the trial ends, the dial shows a 6-digit code and a URL — open it on your phone, enter the code, and pay (credit card or PayPal).

Q. Do I need to buy the five finishes separately?

A. No. Onyx & Gold (Minimal/Data), Rose Gold, Champagne, and Gunmetal & Gold are all unlocked with a single purchase, switchable any time from the watch face settings.

Q. Which watches are compatible?

A. Venu 3, Venu 3S, Forerunner 265, Forerunner 265S, Forerunner 965, fenix 8 AMOLED, fenix 9, fenix 9 Pro, epix Pro (Gen 2) 47mm, and tactix 8 AMOLED.

Q. Does it affect battery life?

A. In the watch's low-power (Always-On) state, Aurum automatically switches to a thin outlined treatment with fewer lit pixels, easing both battery drain and AMOLED burn-in.
