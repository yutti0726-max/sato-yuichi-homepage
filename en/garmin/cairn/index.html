Cairn｜A Garmin Hiking Data Field Built for the Climb

Updated: September 18, 2026

Cairn is a full-screen hiking data field built for climbing, not the flat. The free edition shows six metrics; the paid Cairn Pro expands to twelve, all on one screen.

Short version: the free edition alone gives you six climb-focused metrics — altitude, ascent, grade — and Cairn Pro adds six more when you want them.

One screen, only the numbers that matter on a climb — nothing to scroll for. Every cell drawn the same way — small label, big value — so you read it at a glance.

Price and how to buy

The free Cairn needs nothing beyond installing it — all six metrics are free forever, with no network access and no permissions requested.

Cairn Pro is listed free but is the paid edition. The first 5 activities you use it in are a free trial with every feature working. After that the field shows a 6-digit code and the URL www.kzl.io/code until you unlock it. A one-time payment of US$2.49 (no subscription) unlocks all twelve metrics for good. Open www.kzl.io/code on your phone, enter the code and pay, and it unlocks within a few minutes. Payment is processed by KiezelPay (card or PayPal), not Garmin. Other watches on the same Garmin account don't need a second purchase.

The free Cairn never connects to anything and requests no permissions. Cairn Pro only talks to the KiezelPay server to check unlock status, and never sends altitude, heart-rate, location or any other data anywhere.

FAQ

Q. Is Cairn free to use?

A. Yes. The free Cairn is a six-metric hiking data field and is completely free, with no network access and no permissions requested.

Q. What does Cairn Pro add?

A. Cairn Pro expands to twelve metrics, adding climb rate (VAM), total descent, distance to an ascent goal you set, time until sunset, temperature and watch battery. The first 5 activities are a free trial; after that a one-time US$2.49 payment unlocks it for good.

Q. Where does Cairn Pro's payment go?

A. Payment is processed by the third-party service KiezelPay, not Garmin directly. Once the trial runs out, the field shows a 6-digit code and a URL — open it on your phone, enter the code and pay (credit card or PayPal).

Q. Which watches are supported?

A. Venu 3, Venu 3S, Forerunner 265, 265S, 955, 965, the fenix 6 and 7 series, fenix 8 AMOLED and Solar, fenix 9 and 9 Pro, epix 2 and epix Pro (Gen 2), and enduro/enduro 3 — 30 devices in total.

Q. What's the difference between a data field and a watch face?

A. A data field is a screen you add during an activity recording, such as a run or hike. Cairn and Cairn Pro are data fields, so unlike an always-on watch face such as Aurum, they only appear while an activity is being recorded.
