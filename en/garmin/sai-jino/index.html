Sai and Jino | A Cute Character Digital Watch Face for Garmin That Changes Through the Day

Updated: November 26, 2026

Sai and Jino is a digital watch face for Garmin featuring SIGN's twin map-pin characters: Sai in blue and Jino in orange. The two stand below the time and keep you company through the day. The sky colour, their pictures and their speech bubble change for morning, noon, evening and night.

In short: four scenes through the day, a celebration picture when you reach your step goal, and four data fields for steps, heart rate and more. It is a paid watch face, and every feature is free to try for 24 hours after you install it.

Who is it for?

  • Anyone with a supported Garmin watch who wants a bright face with pictures, not just numbers
  • People who already know Sai and Jino from the LINE stickers
  • Anyone who likes to see on the dial when they reach their step goal
  • Anyone who still wants heart rate, steps and other data on the face

  • When your steps for the day reach your step goal, the two switch to a celebrating picture and say "You did it!". At night they stay in the sleeping picture, even when the goal is reached.
  • The pictures change with the scene; they are not animated.
  • The speech bubble is in Japanese when the watch language is Japanese, and in English otherwise.

On the dial

ItemDetails
TimeLarge digital time (12- or 24-hour, following your watch setting)
DateDate and weekday
TopWatch battery (%) and notification count, shown small
Four data fields (two rows)Choose steps, heart rate, body energy level (BB), stress, calories burned, distance (km or mi, following your watch's units), floors climbed, active minutes (this week), seconds, or off. Large step and calorie counts are shortened (for example 12.3K); a field shows "--" when a value is not available

Settings

  • Swap Sai and Jino (left / right)
  • Speech bubble (on / off)
  • Data field 1-4 (upper left, upper right, lower left, lower right). Defaults: steps, heart rate, BB, stress

Always-on

  • In low-power (always-on) mode, the face shows only the time and the two upper data fields, in grey on black, without the pictures, sky or speech bubble.
  • The display shifts by a few pixels every minute to help reduce the risk of AMOLED burn-in. A seconds field shows "--" in this mode.

Languages

  • The watch face is displayed in English and Japanese. The date, speech bubble and labels are in Japanese when the watch language is Japanese, and in English otherwise.
  • Purchase instructions on the watch are shown in 9 languages, following the watch language (English for other languages).

Price and how to buy

Sai and Jino is a paid watch face (one-time purchase). Every feature is free to try for 24 hours after you install it. There is no subscription. Please check the price on the Connect IQ store page.

How to buy, in three steps:

  1. When the trial ends, the dial shows a QR code and a 6-digit code.
  2. Scan the QR code with your phone (or open www.kzl.io/code and enter the code), then pay once with KiezelPay (card or PayPal).
  3. The face unlocks automatically (this can take a few minutes). Other supported watches on the same Garmin account do not need a second purchase.

Payment is handled by the third-party service KiezelPay, not Garmin.

See How to buy a paid Garmin watch face for the screens on the watch and what to do if it does not unlock.

Sai and Jino uses the network only to check the trial and unlock status with KiezelPay, in the background. Heart rate, steps and other values are only read on the watch for display; the face's own code does not send them anywhere. Location is not used. The Sensor History permission is used to read values such as BB, stress and recent heart rate.

FAQ

Q. Is Sai and Jino free?

A. It is a paid watch face (one-time purchase). Every feature is free to try for 24 hours after you install it. After the trial, one purchase unlocks everything. There is no subscription.

Q. Where do I pay?

A. Payment is handled by KiezelPay, a third-party service, not by Garmin. When the trial ends, the dial shows a QR code and a purchase code; scan the QR code with your phone, or open www.kzl.io/code on your phone or computer and enter the code to pay.

Q. Are the characters animated?

A. No. The pictures change for morning, noon, evening and night, and when you reach your step goal.

Q. What happens if I reach my step goal at night?

A. At night (19:00-4:59) the two stay in the sleeping picture with "Good night", even when the goal is reached.

Q. Can I swap Sai and Jino?

A. Yes, in the settings. You can also turn off the speech bubble.

Q. Do I need to buy it again for another Garmin watch?

A. No. Other supported watches on the same Garmin account do not need a second purchase.
