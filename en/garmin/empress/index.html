Empress | A Frameless Jewelled Chronograph for Garmin

Updated: September 26, 2026

Empress isn't a gold-tinted dial — it's a Garmin watch face set the way a fine jewellery watch is set. The bezel ring is gone, so the dial runs right to the edge of the screen in a chronograph-style layout. It's the third watch face in Yuichi Sato's Garmin lineup, after Aurum's gold and Overwatch's black data-dense design.

Short version: five genuine gemstone finishes unlock with a single purchase, and three subdials keep heart rate, battery and steps in view at all times.

Empress

The finish, in detail

Every dial is built like a piece of jewellery, not a flat gem-coloured fill. Hour markers at 12, 3, 6 and 9 are round cabochons; every other hour is a baguette-cut stone in a polished metal frame. The three subdials are sunburst-finished metal, each rimmed with pavé micro-stones alternating the finish's gemstone with diamonds. The digital time sits in a brushed-metal cartouche. There's no bezel ring at all — just a hairline minute track at the very edge, so the whole screen reads as dial.

Price and how to buy

Everything is free to use for 24 hours after you install it. When the trial ends, a single one-time unlock (US$6.99, no subscription) keeps all features working.

How to buy: once the trial is over the dial shows a 6-digit code and the URL www.kzl.io/code. Open that URL on your phone, enter the code and pay, and the watch face unlocks automatically within a few minutes. Payment is processed by KiezelPay, not Garmin (credit card or PayPal). Once unlocked, other supported watches on the same Garmin account don't need a second purchase.

Empress only talks to the KiezelPay server, to check the unlock status (in the background, roughly once every few minutes). It never sends step, heart-rate or any other personal data anywhere.

FAQ

Q. Is Empress free to use?

A. Everything is free for 24 hours after you install it. After the trial, a single US$6.99 one-time unlock keeps all features working. There is no subscription.

Q. Where does the payment go?

A. Payment is processed by the third-party service KiezelPay, not Garmin directly. When the trial ends, the dial shows a 6-digit code and a URL — open it on your phone, enter the code and pay (credit card or PayPal).

Q. Do I need to buy each finish separately?

A. No. Sapphire, Emerald, Ruby, Amethyst and Diamond White are all unlocked with a single purchase and switchable any time from the settings.

Q. Can I change the moon-phase or seconds display?

A. Yes. The settings let you turn the moon-phase on or off, and choose seconds as digital seconds, a sweep hand, or off entirely.

Q. Which watches are supported?

A. Venu 3, Venu 3S, Forerunner 265, Forerunner 265S, Forerunner 965, fenix 8 AMOLED, fenix 9, fenix 9 Pro, and epix Pro (Gen 2) 47mm.
