Overwatch | A Garmin Watch Face With 5 Layouts on Auto

Updated: September 18, 2026

Most data faces give you one fixed screen and ask you to live with it. Overwatch carries five screens tuned for five different parts of your day, and in Auto mode it switches between them by itself.

Short version: start a run and it becomes a running dashboard, and during your sleep window it goes dark and quiet — no switching required.

Overwatch

Five data layouts in one watch face — and it knows which one you need.

Who is this for?

  • Anyone who mixes running, hiking and other activities
  • Anyone tired of manually switching watch face settings for each activity
  • Anyone who wants Always-On without sacrificing battery life or legibility

The five layouts

LayoutWhat it shows
Cockpit — everything at onceBig time, your Body Battery trend for the last hour, and six fields: heart rate, steps, calories, Body Battery, watch battery and notifications. Twin edge gauges (watch battery / step goal)
Track — while you runBig live pace, heart rate, distance, elapsed time, cadence, average pace and calories, all in one screen while recording an activity
Trail — while you climbBig altitude, time left until sunset, temperature, heart rate, floors climbed, steps and battery
Recovery — the morning afterBig Body Battery with its last hour as a trend line, plus stress, resting heart rate and battery
Night — calm and darkTime only, at the largest size the screen can draw, with date and battery underneath. Built for sleep and Always-On

Price and how to buy

Everything is free to use for 24 hours after you install it. When the trial ends, a single one-time unlock (US$3.99, no subscription) keeps all features working.

How to buy: once the trial is over, the face shows a 6-digit code and the URL www.kzl.io/code. Open that URL on your phone, enter the code and pay, and the watch face unlocks automatically within a few minutes. Payment is processed by KiezelPay, not Garmin (credit card or PayPal). Once unlocked, other supported watches on the same Garmin account don't need a second purchase.

Overwatch only talks to the KiezelPay server, to check the unlock status (in the background, roughly once every few minutes). It never sends step, heart-rate or any other personal data anywhere.

FAQ

Q. Is Overwatch free to use?

A. Everything is free for 24 hours after you install it. After the trial, a single US$3.99 one-time unlock keeps all features working. There is no subscription.

Q. What is Auto mode?

A. Auto mode switches the layout to fit the moment: Track while running, Trail while hiking, Night during your sleep window, Recovery in the hours after you wake, and Cockpit the rest of the time. You can also pin one layout and stay there.

Q. Where does the payment go?

A. Payment is processed by the third-party service KiezelPay, not Garmin directly. When the trial ends, the face shows a 6-digit code and a URL — open it on your phone, enter the code and pay (credit card or PayPal).

Q. Which watches are supported?

A. Venu 3, Venu 3S, Forerunner 265, Forerunner 265S, Forerunner 965, fenix 8 AMOLED (43/47mm), fenix 9 (43/47mm), fenix 9 Pro (43/47/51mm), and epix Pro (Gen 2) 47mm.

Q. Does it affect battery life?

A. In the watch's low-power state, Overwatch drops to a dimmed time-only readout and nudges itself by one pixel every minute, protecting both battery life and AMOLED screens from burn-in.
