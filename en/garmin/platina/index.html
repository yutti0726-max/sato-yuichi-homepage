Platina | A Platinum-Tone Luxury Watch Face for Garmin, 15 Looks

Updated: October 28, 2026

Platina is a luxury analog watch face in cool metal tones, with no gold: platinum-toned hands and applied indices over a guilloche-style colour dial. After the gold Aurum and the jewelled Empress, it's the third fine-watch-style face in Yuichi Sato's Garmin lineup.

In short: five finishes and three layouts (Dress, Moonphase, GMT) give 15 looks in one purchase, switchable any time from the watch face settings.

Platina

Who is this for?

  • Owners of a supported Garmin looking for a silver, blue or green dial instead of gold

  • Anyone who wants heart rate, steps and other data on the dial without spoiling the look

The three layouts

GMTTwo-tone 24-hour ring (night above, day below), lume-style indices and sword hands, date window at 6, 24-hour GMT hand

About the moon phase and the GMT hand

  • The moon phase is an approximation calculated on the watch from the average lunar month (about 29.53 days). It can differ from the actual moon by about a day.
  • The GMT hand shows UTC plus the offset you set, in whole hours from -12 to +14 (default 0 = UTC). It does not follow daylight saving time; change the offset when needed. Half-hour and 45-minute time zones cannot be shown exactly.

Price and how to buy

Platina is a paid watch face. Every feature is free to try for 48 hours after you install it. When the trial ends, a single one-time unlock (US$5.99, no subscription) keeps all features working.

Once the trial is over, the dial shows a purchase code and the URL www.kzl.io/code until the face is bought. Open that URL on your phone or computer, enter the code and pay US$5.99 once, and the face unlocks automatically (this can take a few minutes).

Payment is processed by the third-party service KiezelPay, not by Garmin. The purchase screen on the watch is in English.

Platina uses the network only for the payment service's trial and purchase check, in the background. Heart rate, steps and other values are only read on the watch for display; Platina's own code does not send them anywhere. Location is not used.

Q. How accurate are the moon phase and the GMT hand?

A. The moon phase is an approximation calculated on the watch from the average lunar month (about 29.53 days), so it can differ from the actual moon by about a day. The GMT hand shows UTC plus the offset you set, in whole hours from -12 to +14. It does not follow daylight saving time, so change the offset when needed. Q. What does it show in always-on mode?
