Kichijitsu | A Japanese Lucky-Day Watch Face for Garmin

Updated: October 4, 2026

Is today Taian? Ichiryu Manbai-bi? When is the next Tenshanichi? Kichijitsu is a Garmin watch face that answers at a glance, using the traditional Japanese calendar of lucky days. Switch between three designs in the settings: a shrine-seal Goshuin page, a phase-accurate moon over a night sea, and an Edo-period zodiac clock.

Short version: the Japanese lucky days — rokuyo, Ichiryu Manbai-bi, Tenshanichi and more — are always on your dial, and the whole calendar is calculated on the watch, so it works with no signal at all.

Three designs

DesignWhat it looks like
GoshuinA shrine-seal page on washi paper. Today's rokuyo is stamped as a vermilion seal and the lunar month name is brushed vertically, over a Mt. Fuji watermark. Also shows a countdown to the next lucky day
Moon over the Night SeaThe phase-accurate moon lights a night sea, with a moonlight path that grows brighter toward full moon. Today's rokuyo sits at the top of the ring, the following days run clockwise
Edo WadokeiAn Edo-period clock of the twelve zodiac hours. The current hour glows and a brass pointer turns once a day

Price and how to buy

Everything in the Kichijitsu watch face is free for 24 hours after you install it. After the trial, a single one-time unlock (US$3.99) keeps all features working. All three designs are included, with no subscription or extra charges. Kichijitsu Calendar is completely free.

When the trial ends, the dial shows a 6-digit code and the URL www.kzl.io/code. Open that URL on your phone, enter the code and pay, and the watch face unlocks automatically within a few minutes. Payment is processed by the third-party service KiezelPay, not Garmin (credit card or PayPal).

The calendar is calculated entirely on the watch; it uses no location and no internet. The watch face talks only to KiezelPay's servers, to check the unlock status. Kichijitsu Calendar makes no network connections at all.

FAQ

Q. Is Kichijitsu free to use?

A. Everything is free for 24 hours after you install it. After the trial, a single US$3.99 one-time unlock keeps all features working. There is no subscription. If you only need the list of lucky days, the free Kichijitsu Calendar app shows it.

Q. Where does the payment go?

A. Payment is processed by the third-party service KiezelPay, not Garmin directly. When the trial ends, the dial shows a 6-digit code and a URL — open it on your phone, enter the code and pay (credit card or PayPal).

Q. Do I need to buy each design separately?

A. No. Goshuin, Moon over the Night Sea and Edo Wadokei are all unlocked with a single purchase and switchable any time from the settings.

Q. Does it need internet or location?

A. Not for the calendar. Rokuyo, the lunar date and the lucky days are all calculated on the watch, with data through the end of 2036.

Q. What language is the text in?

A. The calendar text (rokuyo, lunar dates and so on) is in Japanese.
