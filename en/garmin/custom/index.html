Custom Watch Faces for Clubs, Teams and Shops

Updated: November 12, 2026

I make watch faces for Garmin Connect IQ — the dial you see on the watch screen — in the logo and colours of running clubs, teams, races, shops and other groups. I'm Yuichi Sato (SIGN), an independent developer with fourteen watch faces, data fields and apps published on the Connect IQ store, and I handle everything from your first enquiry to submitting the face to the store. This is a new service that I've only just started trying out.

Most projects start from my existing watch face Platina, adapted to your logo and colours. The price is quoted based on what you need. Once it passes review, the finished face is published publicly on the Connect IQ store. It is free on the store, so the people who use it pay nothing.

Who is this for?

  • Running clubs and triathlon teams
  • Sports teams
  • Races and events that want a commemorative face (marathons, trail runs and so on)
  • Outdoor and running shops
  • Golf courses
  • Gyms

I also take requests from individuals. Even then, the finished face is public on the Connect IQ store and appears in store search, so please don't put your name, photos of people or other personal information on the dial.

What I can make

Based on an existing watch face (the main option)

  • I take my published watch face Platina as the base and adapt it to your logo, your group's name and your colours.
  • I don't simply reuse the base design as it is: the layout and finish are changed to suit your group.
  • The items shown (time, date, heart rate, steps and so on) are chosen from what the base face can display.
  • Supported watches and display languages are chosen from what the base face supports, and agreed when I send the quote.

The base, the Platina watch face, supports 49 Garmin watches with a round AMOLED screen, and its display languages are English and Japanese. Up to four data fields can be chosen from heart rate, steps, calories, floors climbed, distance and more. Platina is a paid watch face (one-time purchase), but every feature is free to try for 24 hours after you install it, so you can see how it looks on your own watch before you get in touch. If you want to use a watch that is not in the supported list on the Platina page, such as a watch with an MIP screen, please ask.

Made from scratch

  • If you'd like a face designed from scratch, please ask. Depending on the details, I may not be able to take it on.

How it works

  1. Enquiry: email me with the details listed under "Get in touch" below.
  2. Checking the details: we confirm the logo rights, watches, timing and how you'd like to share the face, by email.
  3. Quote: I send the price, the list of supported watches and the production time.
  4. Making it: I show you preview screens and finish the face with your feedback.
  5. Store review: I submit the face to the Connect IQ store from my own developer account (Yuichi Sato / SIGN), and Garmin reviews it.
  6. Release: once it passes review, the face appears on the store. Your members or customers install it themselves, using the store name you share with them.

How the face is shared

  • The finished face is published on the Connect IQ store from my developer account (Yuichi Sato / SIGN).
  • It is public on the store and appears in store search. I can't promise a way to share it only with your members.
  • The store description will say that the face was made with your group's permission.

What I'll ask of you

  • Confirmation that you can use the logo: please confirm by email that your group owns the logo, name or artwork, or has permission from the owner to use it. When I send the quote, we confirm in writing (email is fine) that your group is responsible for those rights.
  • Logo files: if possible, please send an image with a transparent background, or a format that stays sharp when enlarged.

What I can't take on

  • Logos, names or artwork whose rights can't be confirmed
  • Anything that includes Garmin's name, logos or product names
  • Designs that imitate Garmin's own watch faces or another developer's watch faces
  • Content involving political or religious solicitation, discrimination, adult content or gambling
  • Features that promise medical, health or safety benefits
  • Features that collect personal information or send it anywhere
  • Projects that depend on distributing the face outside the store
  • Deadlines shorter than the time the store review takes

Scope of responsibility

  • I can't guarantee the outcome of the store review or the release date. Garmin carries out the review, and at busy times it can take longer. If you have a deadline, such as a race day, please get in touch with plenty of time to spare.
  • The store may remove a listing at its own discretion, even after release.
  • Changes on Garmin's side (development tools, store rules and so on) that are not covered by the support after release (below) are handled case by case.
  • Supported watches are only those in the list agreed with the quote.
  • Support after release lasts one year from release. It covers fixing display problems on the agreed watches and responding when a change on Garmin's side stops the face working. I fix what I can, and let you know if something can't be fixed. Adding new watches or changing the design is quoted as a separate request.
  • After the year, the face stays on the store. Further fixes are taken on as separate requests. If I ever remove the face from the store, I'll let you know in advance.
  • I can't promise any effect on visitor numbers, sales, health or anything else.

FAQ

Q. How much does it cost?

A. I send a quote based on what you need (based on an existing face or made from scratch, how many watches, which items are shown and so on).

Q. Do the people who use it have to pay?

A. No. The finished face is free on the store.

Q. Can individuals ask too?

A. Yes. The process and what I can't take on are the same as for groups.

Q. Can only our members use it?

A. I can't promise that. The finished face is public on the Connect IQ store and appears in store search.

Q. Which watches will it work on?

A. The watches in the list we agree with the quote. The 49 round AMOLED watches supported by the base face, Platina, are a good guide.

Q. Is it OK if we only want our logo shown?

A. Yes, please ask. If all you need is a photo or logo as the dial background, you may be able to make one yourself with Garmin's Face It app.

Q. Will it be ready by our race day?

A. Store review can take longer at busy times, so I can't promise a release date. If you have a deadline, please get in touch as early as you can. I can't take on requests with a deadline shorter than the time the review takes.
