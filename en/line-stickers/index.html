What kind of stickers?

  • Words for everyday chats: greetings, replies, thanks, apologies, meals, cheering, and feelings.

  • 8 stickers of the two together: a high five, a fist bump, sharing an umbrella, a toast, and more.
  • Big, easy-to-read text: with a white outline, so it reads well on both light and dark chat backgrounds.

Who are Sai & Jino?

They are twin map-pin buddies, born by splitting the brand name "SIGN" in half. Blue Sai (SI) is a reliable supporter who stays quietly by your side. Orange Jino (GN) is an energetic go-getter with a little curl on top of the head.

FAQ

A. You can buy them on the LINE STORE page "Sai & Jino: Everyday Stickers". You can also find them by searching for "Sai & Jino" on the LINE STORE.

Q. How are the illustrations made?

A. Based on the official SIGN mascots Sai & Jino, the poses were created with an AI image generator, and then the text and finishing touches were added.
