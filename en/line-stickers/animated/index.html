What kind of stickers?

  • Replies and caring words: quick reactions like "I get it!" and "So true!", thanks and the reply to thanks ("You're a lifesaver!", "You're welcome"), and caring words for someone who is tired or unwell ("Take it easy", "Get some good rest", "Get well soon").
  • Movements that match the words: nodding, hopping, clapping, shivering, and more. Each of the 16 stickers has its own movement.
  • 7 of Sai, 7 of Jino, 2 of the two together: thoughtful Sai and energetic Jino. "I'm so glad for you~" and "Don't let it get you down" show the two of them together.
  • Different phrases from the static set: the phrases are different from Sai & Jino: Everyday Stickers (40 static stickers), so you can use both sets together.

Who are Sai & Jino?

They are twin map-pin buddies, born by splitting the brand name "SIGN" in half. Blue Sai (SI) is a reliable supporter who stays quietly by your side. Orange Jino (GN) is an energetic go-getter with a little curl on top of the head.

FAQ

Q. Where can I buy them?

A. You can buy them on the LINE STORE page "Animated Sai & Jino: Kind Reactions".

Q. How much are they?

A. The price shown on the LINE STORE page is the price. It may differ depending on where and in which region you buy.

Q. Is this different from the static "Everyday Stickers" set?

A. Yes, it is a separate set. All 16 stickers in "Animated Sai & Jino: Kind Reactions" are animated. The 40 static stickers are a different set, "Sai & Jino: Everyday Stickers", with different phrases.

Q. What language is the text on the stickers?

A. Japanese. The characters' expressions and movements make the meaning easy to guess, too.

Q. How are the illustrations and animations made?

A. Based on the official SIGN mascots Sai & Jino, the poses were created with an AI image generator, and then the text and the animation were added.
