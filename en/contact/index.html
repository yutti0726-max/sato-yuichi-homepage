Contact

For anything about the apps, Garmin watch faces and apps, books or LINE stickers made by SIGN (Yuichi Sato), please use the contact form (Google Forms). You don't need to sign in to a Google account.

Open the contact form

The form opens in a new tab. It is a single form with each question shown in Japanese, English and Chinese.

What you can send

  • Custom work request: for example, a custom watch face for a club, team or shop
  • Bug report: something displays wrongly, doesn't work, crashes and so on
  • Suggestion: ideas that would make something easier to use
  • Request: features you'd like, watches or languages you'd like supported and so on
  • Other: anything that doesn't fit the above

The product name is optional, but please add it if you know it. If you open the form from the button on a product page, the product name is filled in for you. The message field takes up to 3,000 characters.

What to include in a bug report

  • Your device (the model of your smartphone or Garmin watch)
  • The OS version (your Android version, your watch's software version and so on)
  • What you did and what happened (including the wording of any error message)

Whatever you know is fine. These details make it easier to find the cause.

About replies

  • Replies are sent by email.
  • If you'd like a reply, say so in the reply question and enter your email address. If you don't need a reply, you can send the form without an email address.
  • I'll reply once I've looked at the details. Depending on the message, a reply may take some time, or I may not be able to reply.

How your message is handled

What you send is used to reply to you and to improve the products, and for no other purpose. Where it is stored and for how long are set out in the privacy policy. Please don't include addresses, phone numbers or other personal information that the enquiry doesn't need.
