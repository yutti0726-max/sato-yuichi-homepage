Why reconsider leasing now?

A PC lease keeps the upfront cost low, but the monthly payments never stop for as long as the contract runs. A custom-built PC costs more up front but has no ongoing monthly fee after that. Which is actually cheaper depends on your specific situation — but making that call requires comparing the real total cost of each option, not just the sticker price. This book walks through that comparison and the practical steps that follow from it.

FAQ

Q. Which costs less, leasing or buying?

A. The book covers a total-cost-of-ownership (TCO) comparison between leasing and buying, weighing the fixed monthly lease payment against the full cost of buying and maintaining your own PC.

Q. Are there tax advantages to building your own PC for business use?

A. It covers tax-saving techniques using Japan's small-value depreciable-asset rules.

Q. Do I need to be confident choosing parts myself to use this?

A. No — it includes a build table with reliable part choices, so you can follow along even without prior parts knowledge.

Q. Does it cover protecting business data?

A. Yes. It covers NAS-based data protection following the 3-2-1 backup rule.
