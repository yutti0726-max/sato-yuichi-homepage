MY 100 PEAKS | A Hiking Logbook for 100 Peaks

Updated: October 31, 2026

MY 100 PEAKS is a fill-in hiking logbook with one page per peak. From the peak name, elevation, and date to course times, walking time, distance, elevation gain, weather, and a note from the summit, you can keep 100 peaks in one book.

Please note: the book is written in Japanese. The log page fields carry short English labels, but the instructions, notes, and lists are in Japanese.

MY 100 PEAKS: 登山記録ノート（100座の山行を書き残す）

100 log pages, one per peak: date, number of times you have climbed it, a 1–5 rating, peak name, elevation, prefecture, trip style (day hike, mountain hut, tent), partners, weather, trailhead and access, route up and down, course times (8 lines of points and times), walking time, distance, elevation gain and loss, and a note from the summit. Text in Japanese.

Who is this for?

  • Hikers who read Japanese and want to keep a record of each peak in one book
  • Anyone who wants a handwritten record alongside the data from a GPS watch or hiking app
  • Anyone who wants to mark milestones such as their 25th, 50th, or 100th peak

This is a printed paperback (A5, 128 pages): a fill-in logbook you write in by hand. There is no e-book edition. It is a notebook for keeping records; it does not teach climbing skills and does not guarantee your safety in the mountains.

FAQ

Q. What language is the book in?

A. Japanese. The log page fields carry short English labels (for example, MOUNTAIN and ELEVATION) and some section headings are in English, but the instructions, notes, and lists are in Japanese. There is no English edition.

Q. How many peaks can I log?

A. 100, one page per peak. There are also milestone pages after peaks 25, 50, 75, and 100.

Q. Is it tied to a set list of 100 mountains?

A. No. The 100 peaks are the ones you choose and climb yourself. At the back there is a checklist of the highest points of Japan's 47 prefectures, based on data from the Geospatial Information Authority of Japan.

Q. Is there an e-book edition?

A. No. It is a printed paperback (A5, 128 pages) that you write in by hand.
